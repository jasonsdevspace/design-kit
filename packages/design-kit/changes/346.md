A tile in columns keeps its picture and title where a taller tile beside it has them.

- **Sheets.** A tile in columns with a picture starts its picture's line and its text's at its top,
  so a tile stretched to a taller one on its line keeps its picture and title level with that one's,
  and the room it gains falls under its text.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a short tile beside a tall one and holds their pictures and titles
  level.
