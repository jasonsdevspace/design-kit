A tile in columns keeps its title, and its picture, level with a taller tile beside it.

- **Sheets.** A tile in columns starts what it holds at its top, where a row centers its lines in its
  height, so a tile stretched to a taller one on its line keeps its title and picture level with
  that one's, and the room it gains falls under its text. It holds for a tile with a picture and
  one without.
- **Docs.** The reference's tiles in columns say where a short tile's title, picture and extra room
  go beside a tall one.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a short tile beside a tall one, with pictures and without, and
  holds their titles and pictures level.
