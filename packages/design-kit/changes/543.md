A page can take down a form's check and a panel's button by aborting a `signal`, and a panel follows one rule once the button that opened it is let go.

- **Controls.** `attachForm(form, { signal })`, taken down by its signal, takes its listeners off, calls off a refusal not yet landed and a reset's timer still waiting, and takes away the errors it drew, so nothing is said after, not even "Form cleared.", and a refusal asked for after does nothing. The form's check, its `noValidate` and its marks on fields not in error stay while any attach in the form is attached, and go with the last, so the browser checks the form again. `attachPanel(button, { signal })` lets the button go when its signal takes it down, as `detach` does. A signal already aborted attaches nothing to either. An open panel stays the kit's while an attached button names it, so once the button that opened it is let go, Escape closes it as its Close does, with focus going back to the first attached button in the page that names it, where before only the Close did, and the button let go takes `aria-expanded="false"`. A button drawn again while its panel is open, the only one among them too, keeps the panel open and closes it by Escape. A panel's buttons are kept apart from the page, so one in a shadow root takes the panel too. A reset, and a submit the form's own attach checks, take away errors a part's attach drew before it, and a reset calls off a refusal still due, which would otherwise say the errors it took away in place of "Form cleared." The theme switch's listener on the document comes off however its choices close.
- **Docs.** The reference gives `attachForm` and `attachPanel` their `signal` and the panel's rule, and its section on taking a control down names the form and the panels. Record 19 is amended for the panel's rule and record 21 for the form's `noValidate` given back, and record 22 names the form and the panels among the attaches that keep its contract.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox take a form's check down with a refusal and a reset still due and find its timers and frames as before the attach, nothing said and the form as its markup drew it, find the check kept until the last attach in the form is taken down, and find a signal already aborted attaching nothing. Another finds a reset clears the errors a part's attach drew before the form's own attach came, and calls off a refusal still due, on a clock held still. Others let a panel's button go by its signal, close a panel by Escape and by its Close alike once its opener is let go, by Escape once its button is drawn again, the only one among them too, and hand a panel to a button in a shadow root. In Chromium they count the listeners on the form, the panels' buttons and the document and find them as before after an abort, and the theme switch's listener on the document off after each way its choices close.
