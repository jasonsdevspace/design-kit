A refused form says on the status line the name and error of the field it focuses, so a visitor on VoiceOver for iOS hears what is wrong, and a reset that takes away the errors the kit drew says "Form cleared."

- **Controls.** A refusal that moves focus to a field, or finds focus already on it, says the field's name and its error, such as "Player name: Error: Enter a value.", with how many fields need attention where more are wrong. VoiceOver on iOS reads a field's error only as a hint, after a pause, which other speech cuts off and which a visitor who turns hints off never hears, and focus moved by script may not land, so the error is always said. A screen reader that reads the error as focus lands hears it twice. A refusal that moves focus to a summary still says nothing over it. A reset that goes through and takes away errors the kit drew says "Form cleared.", since focus stays on the button and the errors go out of hearing, and a reset with none of the kit's errors to take away, as after a save, says nothing, since errors a page drew are its own to clear.
