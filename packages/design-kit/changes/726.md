A page draws one setting a visitor turns on or off as a checkbox field, a lone box with its label and hint and no group around it, and a form's refusal counts each such box as its own field wherever it stands.

- **Sheets.** A `.dk-field` holding a `label.dk-choice` itself is a checkbox field. Its label hugs its words, its hint starts under the words, past the box, and in a row of fields it takes a line of its own. In forced colors a choice that is off is drawn in `GrayText`.
- **Controls.** A form's refusal takes a checkbox field, a field holding its box's label itself, as its own field even inside a page's own `fieldset` of settings, so one box left unchecked there draws its error above that box alone, marks it alone and names it, where the whole section was taken for one set of choices.
- **React.** `CheckboxField` draws a checkbox field, its label wrapping the box and pointing at it, its error before the label and its hint after it, both describing the box. With `readOnly` it draws the box's state in words, "On" or "Off" unless `onWords` and `offWords` give others, with a hidden field carrying its value while it is checked.
- **Docs.** The reference gains a section on the checkbox field, a `CheckboxField` props table, and a pointer from the choice fields and from the words a refusal uses for a box that stands alone.
- **Tests.** A refused submit in a page's own section of two required boxes draws one error, above the unchecked box. Browser tests in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit find the box named by its label and described by its hint, the error above the box with focus on it, in a form written by hand and in one React draws, the label a target of the field's height and a thumb's on a phone, the hint starting under the words and never toggling the box, no audit failure in any state, and a long label wrapping at 320 wide and twice the text size. In forced colors an invalid box has a dashed line and one that is off is drawn in `GrayText`.
