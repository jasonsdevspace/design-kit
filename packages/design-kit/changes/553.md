A group the arrow keys move through places its stop for the Tab key as it comes into sight, so it can be attached while it is hidden.

- **Controls.** `attachRoving`, and `useRoving` in React, place the group's stop again as the group comes into sight, by watching its size, which grows from nothing as it shows. A row of tabs attached in a closed `details`, a hidden panel or a dialog not yet open used to leave every cell at `tabindex="-1"` once it showed, so a keyboard user tabbed past it. The group's detach stops watching it.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on tabs and on a grid of buttons, and `attachRoving`'s comment, say the stop is placed as the group comes into sight.
- **Tests.** Browser tests attach a row of tabs in a closed `details`, open it and reach the chosen tab by Tab, and attach a grid in a hidden panel whose first cell is hidden on its own, show the panel and reach the first cell in sight by Tab.
