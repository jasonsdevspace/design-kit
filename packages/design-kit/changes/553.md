A group the arrow keys move through places its stop for the Tab key as it comes into sight, so it can be attached while it is drawn at no size.

- **Controls.** `attachRoving`, and `useRoving` in React, place the group's stop again as the group's size changes, so a group attached in a closed `details`, under `hidden` or `display: none`, or in a dialog not yet open has its stop once it shows. Such a row of tabs used to leave every cell at `tabindex="-1"` once it showed, so a keyboard user tabbed past it. A cell a media query or a page's rule hides as it resizes the group hands the stop on to the next cell, as a cell taken out does. A group under `inert`, or hidden in a way that keeps its size, is placed again only as its cells change or are reached. The group's detach stops watching its size.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on tabs and on a grid of buttons, and `attachRoving`'s comment, say the stop is placed as the group comes into sight from no size.
- **Tests.** Browser tests attach a row of tabs in a closed `details`, open it and reach the chosen tab by Tab, attach a grid in a hidden panel whose first cell is hidden on its own, show the panel and reach the first cell in sight by Tab, and attach a row of tabs in a dialog, open it with `showModal()` and reach the chosen tab by Tab, each finding the stop placed before Tab is pressed. Another hides a cell that had focus by a class outside its group and finds the stop on the next cell, and a React test opens a details around a row given `useRoving` and reaches its chosen tab by Tab.
