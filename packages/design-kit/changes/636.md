In Safari a page that sets its root font size keeps the room under a toast and over a tab bar that
its text size asks for.

- **Upgrading.** A page that sets `--dk-target`, `--dk-target-large`, `--dk-space-1`,
  `--dk-space-4` or `--dk-text-caption` on its root, and wants the room the root keeps under a
  toast or over a tab bar to follow, sets `scroll-padding-bottom` on its root in a rule of its own.
  The root's rules read the kit's own numbers for those lengths, in em, where they read the tokens,
  so a token a page sets there moves every piece as before and the root's scroll padding stays.
- **Sheets.** The root's `scroll-padding-bottom` under a shown toast on a page with no frame, and
  over a tab bar that ends a page, is written in em through working values in the tokens sheet that
  hold each token's number, where it was written in the tokens' rem. Safari draws a rem in a
  property of the root itself from 16px whatever size the page sets there, so with
  `html { font-size: 200% }` it kept 114px under a toast 186px tall and 73px over a bar 113px tall,
  and Tab set a link wholly under each. It keeps 226px and 146px, as Chromium and Firefox do. A
  page at the usual root size, and every other browser, draws as before.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox double the root size in the page's own
  sheet, Tab to a link under a shown toast and under a tab bar at 390 and 320 wide, and find the
  link clear of each and the room kept no less than what covers it. A sheet test finds no rem,
  written or read through a token, in a property of the root's own, and each twin in em holding
  its token's number and read by the root alone. The tab bar's browser tests run in Firefox as well
  as Chromium and WebKit.
