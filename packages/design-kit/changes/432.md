A grid of buttons whose columns asked for do not fit draws the most that fit and divide its cells
evenly, so six asked for in six columns draw as two rows of three on a phone, never five and one.

- **Sheets.** Where the columns asked for do not fit, a grid of four, six, eight, nine, ten or
  twelve cells draws the most columns that fit and divide its cells, two where none larger does,
  and one where not even two do, and a grid of fewer cells than the columns asked for stays in one
  row wherever they all fit. The grid counts its cells again as a page adds or takes one away.
  A grid of any other count draws as many as fit, as before, and where the columns asked for fit,
  the grid draws them. The sheets' budget rises from 10.75 KB to 11.5 KB, set again by the size
  audit's rule.
- **Docs.** The reference says which counts of cells a grid evens out and how.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox check the columns a grid of each count
  draws from 120px to 640px against the rule, in a grid, a tall grid and at twice the text size,
  with each cell at its track's share and none wider than the grid, one column where one is asked
  for, the count taken again as a cell is taken away and added, six asked for in six drawn as three
  on a 320px phone and at twice the text size on a 390px phone, and four cells in one row.
