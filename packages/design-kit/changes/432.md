A grid of buttons whose columns asked for do not fit draws the most that fit and divide its cells
evenly, so six asked for in six columns draw as two rows of three on a phone, never five and one.

- **Sheets.** Where the columns asked for do not fit, a grid of four, six, eight, nine, ten or
  twelve cells draws the most columns that fit and divide its cells, or two where none larger does,
  and a grid of fewer cells than the columns asked for stays in one row at each cell's even share.
  A grid of any other count draws as many as fit, as before, and where the columns asked for fit,
  the grid draws them. The sheets' budget rises from 10.75 KB to 11.5 KB, set again by the size
  audit's rule.
- **Docs.** The reference says which counts of cells a grid evens out and how.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox check the columns a grid of each count
  draws across phone and tablet widths against the rule, every cell at the target, six asked for in
  six drawn as three on a 320px phone and at twice the text size on a 390px phone, and four cells
  in one row.
