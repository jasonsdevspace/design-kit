The browser tests' still clock holds on a runner however slow it is between putting the clock on and
stopping it.

- **Tests.** The still clock the browser tests share starts at a fixed moment and stops a day after
  it, so a runner slow between the two calls never fails a test with "Cannot fast-forward to the
  past" before its first step. A test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox waits seconds between the two
  calls and finds the clock stopped. The checked form's spec says why its React page's radio is
  chosen by the keyboard, with what is known of a click in Firefox that once left it unchanged.
