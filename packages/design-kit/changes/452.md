A refused form moves focus to its first error and says once what needs attention.

- **Controls.** `attachForm(form, { status, say, summary })` returns what refuses a submit once the page has drawn its errors: a frame later focus moves to the summary, the first field marked invalid, or a radio set's checked radio, and one message is said, how many fields need attention, or the error's own words where the field already had focus.
- **React.** `useFormRefusal()` returns a ref for the form and what refuses it, speaking on the screen's `StatusProvider`.
- **Docs.** The reference's Fields section shows the refused form with the helper.
- **Tests.** Browser tests refuse with focus elsewhere and hear the count, refuse with focus on the field and hear its error, twice, focus a radio set's checked radio and a named summary, and refuse a React form through the screen's status line.
