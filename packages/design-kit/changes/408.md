A panel stays as it is when an off button is pressed or a second button is attached.

- **Controls.** A press of an attached button with `aria-disabled="true"` leaves its panel alone,
  as a stepper's off button does. A button attached while another attached button already names
  the panel follows the panel as it is, so a button drawn later never closes a panel the visitor
  opened. The first button for a panel still starts it as its markup says.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close and the controls' doc comment say both. The reference's
  frame and band insets name `--dk-app-side`, and its tab bar draws its icons as kit icons, with
  `dk-icon`, so they take the kit's stroke.
- **Tests.** Browser tests press an off button and find its panel unchanged, and attach a second
  button to an open panel and find it open and the button expanded.
