A stepper speaks through a page's own `say`, takes options that may be undefined, and keeps its buttons when a page attaches it again as focus leaves.

- **Controls.** `attachStepper` takes `say`, as the form, the busy button and progress do, so a React page puts a stepper's words on the screen's line through `useStatus`. `status` and `words` accept undefined, as every other attach's options do. A stepper detached and attached again from a page's own focusout handler keeps its buttons, since the timer its focusout sets is cleared as it is let go and marks nothing once it is.
- **Docs.** The reference names `say`, shows it from React, and says a stepper's words on a page's line leave as any message there does.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox hear a press through a page's own `say` and keep a stepper's buttons after a focusout handler attaches it again. The type smoke test passes a line and words that may be undefined.
