A stepper speaks through a page's own `say`, takes options that may be undefined, and keeps its buttons when a page attaches it again as focus leaves.

- **Controls.** `attachStepper` takes `say`, as the form, the busy button and progress do, so a React page puts a stepper's words on the screen's line through `useStatus`. `status` and `words` accept undefined, as every other attach's options do. A stepper detached and attached again from a page's own focusout handler keeps its buttons, since the timers its focusout and a reset set are cleared as it is let go and do nothing once it is. A stepper moved from a page's line to `say` takes its words back from the line, and `say` is the one used where both are given. Words said through `say` leave in their time, since the stepper cannot take them back from a function, and a press's words not yet said are dropped once the value is typed over.
- **Docs.** The reference names `say`, shows it from React, and says a stepper's words on a page's line leave as any message there does.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox hear a press through a page's own `say` keep a stepper's buttons after a focusout handler or a reset is followed by attaching it again, and take a stepper's words back from a line it no longer speaks on. The type smoke test passes a line and words that may be undefined.
