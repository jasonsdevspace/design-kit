A React page speaks on its status line through the kit, and words that change with each press can wait until they stop changing.

- **Controls.** The function `attachStatus` returns takes `{ settle }`, a wait in milliseconds for words to stop changing before they are said, and any message said on the line drops words still waiting, except polite words, which give way to them.
- **React.** `StatusProvider` draws a screen's status line and attaches it, and `useStatus()` returns the function that says a message on it from any part under the provider, the same across renders, holding words said before the line is attached. A provider inside another takes the calls under it on a line of its own.
- **Docs.** The reference's Status line section states the policy every speaking piece follows and shows the provider and the hook, and record 15 notes the settle.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find words that settle said once they stop changing, waiting words dropped by another message, the same words twice heard twice, words said before a provider's line is attached held and said, the hook's function the same across renders, and a nested provider's own line. Unit tests find the provider's line drawn as the status line's markup and a part with no provider refused.
