A stepper tells a screen reader its range and its limits, and a page can say its own words after a press in the same message.

- **Controls.** Once attached, a stepper describes its field by its range in words, such as "From 0 to 10.", after the page's own description and from a `dk-stepper-limits` span it fills, and follows the limits as they change. A press that lands on a limit says so with the value, as "10, the most". `attachStepper` takes `words`, a function of the value read as the message is put on the line, and `status`, a page's own status line to say each press on in place of the stepper's.
- **React.** `StepperField` draws the `dk-stepper-limits` span.
- **Docs.** The reference's Stepper section describes the range words, the limit words and both options, and record 13 sets the controls' budget at 8.75 KB by its rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find the range in the field's description after a hint and following a new range, the limit words on a press, and a page's words said after the value on the page's own line. The controls are held to 8.75 KB gzipped.
