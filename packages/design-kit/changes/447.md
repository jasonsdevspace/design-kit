A stepper tells a screen reader its range and its limits, and a page can say its own words after a press in the same message.

- **Controls.** Once attached, a stepper describes its field by its range in words, such as "From 0 to 10." or "Only 3.", after the page's own description and from a `dk-stepper-limits` span it fills, and follows the limits as they change, with the error in their place while it shows, whoever showed it. A press that lands on a limit, or on the last value a step reaches short of the greatest, says so with the value, as "10, the most", and that button goes off. `attachStepper` takes `words`, a function of the value read as the message is put on the line, and `status`, a page's own line to say each press on and keep there in place of the stepper's. Typing over the value, resetting the form or taking the stepper away clears only what the stepper said and leaves a waiting message unsaid.
- **React.** `StepperField` draws the `dk-stepper-limits` span.
- **Docs.** The reference's Stepper section describes the range words, the limit words and both options, and says a page no longer states the range in its hint. Record 13 sets the controls' budget at 8.75 KB by its rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find the range in the field's description after a hint and following a new range, the limit words on a press and at a step short of the greatest, an error the page shows kept, a page's words said after the value on the page's own line, and only the stepper's words cleared from it. The site's markup test reads the range words as the markup draws them. The controls are held to 8.75 KB gzipped.
