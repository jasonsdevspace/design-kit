A frame pictured inside an inert element no longer draws the page around it as an app's.

- **Sheets.** A frame, a page that ends in a tab bar or a shown toast inside an `inert` element, such
  as a picture of a screen on a content page, is not the page's own. The page around it keeps the
  column's bands for its strip, header, content and footer, a dialog keeps a page's card inset and
  targets, pull to refresh stays on, and the page keeps no room at its foot for the pictured toast.
- **Docs.** The reference's app frame says a frame pictured inside an inert element is not the
  page's own.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a content page plain and with an inert picture of a frame, a page
  that ends in a tab bar and a shown toast, and holds the strip, the content, the footer, the
  dialog and the page's foot the same.
