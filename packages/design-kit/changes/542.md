A page can take down a status line and a toast by aborting a `signal`, under the teardown contract of record 22.

- **Controls.** `attachStatus(line, { signal })` lets the line go on abort, as `StatusProvider` does when it unmounts: its timers are cleared, its words dropped and it is emptied, no piece says anything on it until it is attached again, and the function the attach returned stays silent for good. `attachToast(element, { signal })` dismisses its message on abort without moving focus, takes off its listeners, its observer, its listener on the window's size and its timer, takes away a tabindex it added and lets its line go, and the function it returned, and what that returned, do nothing after. A signal already aborted returns a function that says or shows nothing, and a piece already attached returns its first attach's function and does not hear the later call's signal. The form, a busy button and progress now speak on a page's line without attaching it, as the stepper and the copy button already did, so a page that attaches the line with a signal after they have spoken owns it. A toast listens to the window's size once however many messages it shows.
- **Docs.** Record 15 is amended for the status line's and the toast's signal, and record 22 names them among the pieces that keep the contract. The reference gives `attachStatus` and `attachToast` their `signal` and says how the signal alone takes each down.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox attach a status line and a toast with a signal already aborted and find nothing said or shown, abort a signal and find the line emptied and silent for the page's function, a stepper and progress, find a line those spoke on first let go of by the page's signal, find a toast dismissed without focus sent back, its tabindex taken away and a later message unshown, and find a later attach's signal unheard. They count the timers and observers running before attaching and after aborting and find them equal, and in Chromium they count the listeners on the toast's elements and on the window, and find every one the attach added off after an abort.
