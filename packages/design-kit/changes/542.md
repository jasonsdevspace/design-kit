A page can take down a status line and a toast by aborting a `signal`, under the teardown contract of record 22.

- **Controls.** `attachStatus(line, { signal })` lets the line go on abort, as `StatusProvider` does when it unmounts: its timers are cleared, its words dropped and it is emptied, no piece says anything on it until it is attached again, and the function the attach returned says nothing after. A line let go of and attached afresh takes its old attach down, so its signal holds nothing, while a piece given the line itself speaks on its next attach. `attachToast(element, { signal })` dismisses its message on abort as its Close does, focus in it going back where a dismiss sends it, takes off its listeners, its observer, its listener on the window's size and its timer, takes away a tabindex it added that the page has not changed since, and lets its line go, and the function it returned, and what that returned, do nothing after. A signal already aborted returns a function that says or shows nothing, though a toast missing a part is still refused, and a piece already attached returns its first attach's function and does not hear the later call's signal. The form, a busy button and progress now speak on a page's line without attaching it, as the stepper and the copy button already did, so a page that attaches the line with a signal after they have spoken owns it. A toast listens to the window's size once however many messages it shows.
- **Docs.** Record 15 is amended for the status line's and the toast's signal, and record 22 names them among the pieces that keep the contract. The reference gives `attachStatus` and `attachToast` their `signal` and says how the signal alone takes each down. Record 13 sets the controls' budget at 18.5 KB, from 17.75 KB, once moving the status line, the toast, the form, the panels and the remaining attaches onto the helper took them to 18,269 bytes together, with a median function of 606 bytes.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox attach a status line and a toast with a signal already aborted and find nothing said or shown, abort a signal and find the line emptied and silent for the page's function, a stepper and progress, find a line those spoke on first let go of by the page's signal, find a piece given the line speak again once it is attached again, find a toast taken down with focus on its Close send focus back where it came from and a later message unshown, the tabindex it added taken away and one the page set since kept, and find a later attach's signal unheard. They count the timers and observers running before attaching and after aborting and find them equal, and in Chromium they count the listeners on the toast's elements and on the window, and find every one the attach added off after an abort. Unit tests find a line let go of and attached afresh take its old attach down, leaving nothing on that attach's signal, and a toast missing a part refused whatever its signal.
