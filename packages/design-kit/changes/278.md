A content page is spaced by one class.

- **Upgrading.** `dk-explainer-lead` is held to the measure wherever it sits, so a lead with it
  outside an explainer is no wider than `--dk-measure`.
- **Sheets.** `dk-page` on a content page's main content spaces what it holds by the flow and its
  sections `--dk-space-7` apart with no rule between them, sets its `h1` at the display size and a
  section's `h2` at the title size, starts its text at the strip's inset by the band insets, and
  holds its prose to the measure. `dk-lead` is the lead under a name of its own, held to the
  measure, with `dk-explainer-lead` the same rule.
- **Docs.** The reference shows a content page and its lead, and decision 0017 says why a content
  page is a container of its own.
- **Tests.** A content page spaces its blocks by the flow and its sections further apart, starts its
  text at the strip's inset, sizes its headings, holds its prose and lead to the measure beside a
  table at the column's width, takes its controls in the markup's order, and cuts nothing off at 320
  wide, twice the text size and the wider text spacing.
