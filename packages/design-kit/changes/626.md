A stepper's failure in a refused form shows one error and is said once, in React as in plain markup.

- **Upgrading.** `invalid` can be handed an empty list, where the only fields that fail are steppers
  showing their own error, and the refusal still follows. A page does not take an empty list for a
  pass, nor read the first failure as present. A summary built from the failures does not hold such
  a stepper. The form's `errorWords` and a field's `data-error-*` words do not reach a stepper in
  React, whose error takes its words from `rangeError`.
- **Controls.** `attachForm` leaves a stepper that shows its own range error out of the failures it
  hands `invalid`, so a form refused through `useFormRefusal` draws and describes the stepper's
  error alone, where it drew the form's "Enter 10 or less." above it and said both. The refusal
  still counts the stepper and names it. An error shown in the stepper's field that is not the
  stepper's keeps the field at a submit, and the failure is drawn there or handed to `invalid`. A
  form that checks a submit quiets every attached stepper in it, on the stepper's own line, on a
  page's line it was given and through a page's `say`, so a value typed past its range and submitted
  by Enter is said by the refusal alone, or not at all where focus moves to a summary, where the
  stepper's line and the page's line both changed in one moment.
- **Docs.** The reference's status line section says a field's error is not live and a refused form
  says one message, where it said a field's error is never said on the line. Its refused form and
  stepper sections, and records 20 and 21, say which error is drawn, whose words it takes and who
  says it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox type a value past a stepper's range and
  submit by Enter, in a form attached with `attachForm` and one refused through `useFormRefusal`,
  and find one error drawn, one in the field's description and one message on one line, with the
  stepper named, with another field named, with a summary taking focus and with a stepper that
  speaks through a `say` of its own. They find a page's own error in a stepper's field left as the
  one error.
