A stepper's failure in a refused form shows one error and is said once, in React as in plain markup.

- **Upgrading.** `invalid` can be handed an empty list, where the only fields that fail are steppers
  showing their own error, and the refusal still follows. A page does not take an empty list for a
  pass, nor read the first failure as present. A summary is built from the second list `invalid` is
  handed, which holds a stepper whose own range error shows with that error's words, since one built
  from the first list would leave the stepper out and nothing would say it.
- **Controls.** `attachForm` leaves a stepper whose own range error shows out of the failures it
  hands `invalid`, so a page refused through `useFormRefusal` shows and describes the stepper's
  error alone, where it drew the form's "Enter 10 or less." above it and the refusal said both. The
  refusal still counts the stepper and names it. As it refuses, the form takes back a failing
  stepper's own words not yet said, on the stepper's own line, on a page's line and through a page's
  `say`, so a value typed past its range and submitted by Enter or a press on the button is said by
  the refusal alone, or not at all where focus moves to a summary, where the stepper's line and the
  page's line both changed in one moment.
- **Docs.** The reference's status line section says a field's error is not live and a refused form
  says one message, where it said a field's error is never said on the line. Its refused form and
  stepper sections, and records 20 and 21, say which error is drawn, whose words it takes and who
  says it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox type a value past a stepper's range and
  submit by Enter and by a press on the button, in a form attached with `attachForm` and one refused
  through `useFormRefusal`, and find one error drawn, one in the field's description and one message
  on one line, with the stepper named, with another field named, with a summary taking focus and
  with a stepper that speaks through a `say` of its own. They find the stepper's own words left said
  in a form the kit does not check and on a stepper the check passes over.
