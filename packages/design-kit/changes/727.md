A page that draws only some of the React components and hooks sends only their code, even where another page of the same site draws the rest.

- **React.** Each hook and each component, or a few that share their parts, is a module of its own that `./react` gathers, so a site's bundler no longer puts every hook and every component's markup in a chunk all its pages load. A page that keeps a status line, a skip link and the theme switch beside screens that refuse a form, move through a group of cells and draw a stepper, a toast and a checkbox field sends none of their code. What `./react` exports is unchanged.
- **Docs.** Record 20 says each hook lives in a module of its own and the lint holds every module of markup components to no effect.
- **Tests.** A build test bundles a site of two pages from the package as npm ships it and holds a page that keeps a status line to none of the form's, the group's, the stepper's, the toast's or the checkbox field's code, and a page alone that keeps a status line or a busy button to none of the other pieces. The test that the React modules reach the kit only through the entry reads every module, and the lint that keeps effects out of the markup covers each module of components.
