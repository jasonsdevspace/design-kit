A raised panel and a tile cast a soft shadow where the page sets none.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-shadow-card` is the soft lift of a raised panel and a tile, deeper in the dark
  theme.
- **Sheets.** A raised panel and a tile cast the card shadow, where a page sets no shadow of its own
  by `--dk-panel-shadow` or `--dk-tile-shadow`.
- **Tests.** A raised panel and a tile cast the card shadow with none set, in both themes and none
  in forced colors, while a plain panel casts none.
