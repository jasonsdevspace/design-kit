A raised panel casts a soft shadow where the page sets none, and a tile casts none.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-shadow-resting` is the soft lift of a raised panel, deeper in the dark theme.
- **Sheets.** A raised panel casts the resting shadow where a page sets no shadow of its own by
  `--dk-panel-shadow`. A tile casts none unless a page sets one by `--dk-tile-shadow`, so the raised
  panel above a list stays the one thing lifted.
- **Tests.** A raised panel casts the resting shadow with none set, in both themes and none in
  forced colors, while a plain panel and a tile cast none.
