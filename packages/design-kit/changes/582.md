A toast hands focus to the page's body in every engine where nothing else takes it, and back to the
control inside a frame of the page's own origin that had it.

- **Controls.** `attachToast` moves focus off the toast before it hands focus back, so where nothing
  it tries takes focus, focus falls to the page's body in WebKit and Firefox as it does in Chromium,
  rather than staying on the toast's hidden button. Focus a page or an action moves elsewhere in the
  same task stays. Where focus was inside a frame of the page's own origin as the latest message
  came, focus goes back to the control inside the frame rather than to the frame. In a frame of
  another origin, focus goes back to the frame itself, as near as a page can reach.
- **Docs.** The reference's toast section and record 23 say where focus goes where nothing takes it
  and where it was inside a frame, of the page's own origin or another, and record 23 gives the
  controls' new weight.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find focus on the page's body as a press
  on a toast's action is handled where nothing it would hand focus to takes it, focus an action
  moves elsewhere staying, focus back on a button inside a frame of the page's own origin, and focus
  on a frame of another origin.
