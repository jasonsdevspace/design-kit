A toast lets focus fall to the page's body in every engine where nothing else takes it, and sends it
on to the control inside a frame of the page's own origin that last had focus there.

- **Controls.** `attachToast` hands focus back before it hides its buttons, and where nothing it
  tries takes focus, it moves focus off the toast, so focus falls to the page's body in WebKit and
  Firefox as it does in Chromium, rather than staying on the toast's hidden button. Where focus goes
  back to a frame of the page's own origin that had focus as the latest message came, it goes on to
  the control inside that last had focus there. A frame of another origin keeps focus itself, as
  near as a page can reach, though Firefox does not focus such a frame while a key is being pressed,
  so a dismiss by keyboard there goes on to the body of the app's frame or the main content.
- **Docs.** The reference's toast section and record 23 say where focus goes where nothing takes it
  and where it goes back to a frame, of the page's own origin or another, record 23 gives the
  controls' new weight, and the reference says a main content takes focus by the `tabindex="-1"`
  the skip link gives it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find focus on the page's body as a press
  on a toast's action is handled where nothing it would hand focus to takes it, focus left where a
  page's listener moves it as focus leaves the action, focus sent on to the button inside a frame of
  the page's own origin that last had focus there, and focus on a frame of another origin after a
  press by pointer. In Firefox, a test finds a dismiss by Enter sending focus to the main content
  where that frame cannot take it.
