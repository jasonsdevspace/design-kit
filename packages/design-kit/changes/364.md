A tile in a panel keeps every word whole on a phone at twice the text size.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-card-inset` at 48rem wide or less is `--dk-space-4-held`, 1rem and no more than
  16px at any text size, where it grew with the text, so a panel and the tiles in it leave a tile's
  words room on a phone at twice the text size. At the default text size nothing moves, and a raised
  panel's words and the tiles under it still start on one line.
- **Docs.** The reference's tokens and panels say the card inset holds at 16px on a phone.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws tiles in a plain and a raised panel on a content page at 320 wide
  and twice the text size, and finds no word drawn across two lines.
