A press in a frame of the page's origin inside one of another origin, made while that outer frame's own load is still to come, focuses the heading of the view it brings in Firefox too, now that focus there is read where it is.

- **Docs.** Record 19 says again that the walk on a blur hears a frame before the click that moved focus into it in every engine measured, and that the read on that blur goes through a frame of another origin to the inner frame that has focus.
- **Tests.** The browser test for such a press is no longer marked as failing in Firefox, and finds the view's heading focused in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
