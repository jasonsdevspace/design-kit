A toast, a dialog or a panel hands focus back from a control inside a shadow tree within it, as it
does from a plain control.

- **Controls.** The rule the toast, the dialog and the panels share for handing focus back counts
  focus inside a shadow tree within a piece as focus in it, by walking out through each shadow
  root's host, so a page's own button component in a dialog's form, a panel or a toast's buttons
  gets focus handed back from it. The toast reads focus there as focus in it too, so its wait holds
  while focus is there and a message shown then keeps where focus was before.
- **Docs.** The reference's section on taking a control down and record 23 say focus inside a shadow
  tree within a piece counts as focus in it, and record 23 gives the controls' new weight.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find focus back on the opener once a
  dialog is taken down and once a panel is closed by Escape with focus on a button inside a shadow
  tree within them, and focus taken out of a toast dismissed from one where nothing on its list
  takes focus.
