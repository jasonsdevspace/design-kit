An app frame's sides read one named property, and the larger icons share one stroke.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-app-side` is how far an app frame's words stand in from its sides, the held
  step, and a toast's sides and the band insets on an app's page read it.
- **Sheets.** An icon-only button's icon and a tab bar's are stroked by one rule, at two in twenty
  four of their own size, so an icon-only button's is drawn a little lighter, two in twenty four
  of 1.25rem rather than an eighth of its button's text.
- **Docs.** The reference's token table lists `--dk-app-side`, and record 13 records the sheets'
  weight against the median piece.
- **Tests.** The browser tests share their measures of a container's foot, the lines of text, a
  token's length and a page that loads the controls, and compare with tokens where a relation is
  meant. The token tests read their lists by what they hold rather than by order.
