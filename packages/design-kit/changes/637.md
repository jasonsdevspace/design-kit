A stepper is as tall as the fields beside it on a wide page, so a row's labels stand level.

- **Upgrading.** On a page wider than 48rem, outside an app's frame, a page that ends in a tab bar
  and their dialogs, a stepper is `--dk-field-height` tall, 36 pixels, where it was 44, so what
  follows it sits 8 pixels higher. Its buttons keep their width. A page that wants the larger
  target there gives the stepper `dk-stepper-tall`, `tall` in React. A phone, an app and a tall
  stepper are drawn as before.
- **Sheets.** A `dk-stepper`'s buttons and its `dk-stepper-value` set no height of their own, so
  each takes the height every button and field takes: `--dk-field-height` above 48rem, and
  `--dk-target` at 48rem or less, in an app's frame, on a page that ends in a tab bar and in a
  dialog on either. In a `dk-fields` row a stepper's label and control stand level with those of
  the fields beside it at every width, where the label stood 8 pixels higher on a wide page. A
  read-only stepper and an inline one are as tall as any other, so a stepper turned read only keeps
  its height. A stepper's button keeps no padding, so its figure stands inside a field's height.
  `dk-stepper-tall` keeps the larger target at every width.
- **Docs.** The reference says where a stepper is the field's height and where the target's, that
  its buttons are the target wide at both, and that a row of fields stands a stepper's label level
  with the labels beside it. Record 26 gives why the stepper takes the height every control takes,
  with leaving it the target tall, a row that lines its fields up by their labels and a height of
  the stepper's own turned down. The accessibility notes give the buttons' width.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox draws a stepper beside a text field in
  a `dk-fields` row at 1280 wide, on a page, in a panel and in a dialog, and finds their labels and
  controls level, the stepper's buttons and value at the field's height and the buttons the target
  wide. It finds the same pair level at the target's height on a phone, at 48rem, in an app's
  frame, on a page that ends in a tab bar and in a dialog on either, a tall stepper at the larger
  target at both widths, and an inline stepper and a read-only one at the field's height at each.
