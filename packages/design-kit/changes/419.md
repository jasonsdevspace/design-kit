A panel hands focus back only where focus is still where a press on a control in it left it.

- **Controls.** The kit notes where focus was as a visitor's press landed, before the page's own
  handlers for it ran, and a closing panel hands focus to its button only where focus is still
  there after a press on a control in the panel. So a link whose page moves focus to the `main`
  that holds the panel keeps it there, and a close from the page's code, or a click its script
  gives the Close, after a tap on the panel's plain text moves no focus. In Safari focus may
  already sit where a page means to put it, so a page that moves focus as it closes a panel moves
  it after `set(false)`.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close and record 13 say so, and the controls' budget is 7.75 KB,
  set by the budgets' rule with the rule in place.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel by a link that moves focus to the `main` that holds it,
  before closing in Chromium and after closing in every engine, and from the page's code and by
  its script's click on the Close after a press on the panel's plain text, and find focus where
  the page or the browser left it.
