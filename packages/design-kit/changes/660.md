`ValueField` draws a value of words a visitor reads but cannot change, sized to its value so it
wraps and shows whole at a large text size, and a read-only select draws its words the same way.

- **Upgrading.** A read-only `InputField` is one line and cuts off words that run past its edge, as
  "Round 3 of 10" does at 320 wide with twice the text size and the wider text spacing. A page that
  shows a value of words read only draws it with `ValueField`, or writes
  `<textarea class="dk-textarea dk-value" rows="1" readonly>` in its field. A read-only
  `InputField` stays for a short figure and for a field held read only for a moment while it saves.
  A read-only `SelectField` draws that textarea in place of an input, so Enter in it does not
  submit the form, and a page's own rule for its `dk-input` does not reach it.
- **Sheets.** `dk-value` on a `dk-textarea` fills its field, takes `field-sizing: content` with no
  resize handle where the browser has it, and takes the block padding that centers one line in a
  field's height, the target's on a phone and in an app's frame, so a value of one line stands as
  tall as a read-only field beside it with its words level. A value in a `dk-field-action` row
  keeps the room the button leaves, as an input does.
- **React.** `ValueField`, with `ValueFieldProps`, draws the textarea with its label, hint, error
  and action, one row and read only, its `ref` the textarea. A read-only `SelectField` draws the
  chosen option's words in it.
- **Docs.** The reference gives `ValueField` a section of its own, with its markup and its props,
  and says a read-only `InputField` is for a short figure or a field held read only for a moment.
  Its example of read-only fields draws the round as a value, a score as a read-only field and a
  game whose chosen option's words are long. The accessibility notes say why a read-only text field
  can be cut off, what a screen reader may call a value, and how a browser that cannot size a field
  to its content draws one. Record 28 gives the reasons and the options turned down.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox stand a value of one line at a read-only
  field's height with its first line level with the field's words at 1280 and 390 wide and in an
  app's frame, at the usual and twice the text size, grow a long value and a long option to show
  whole at 320 wide with twice the text size and wider spacing, draw a value one field tall where
  the browser cannot size it, fill a value's field and keep a button's place beside it, keep a
  note's height as it turns read only, and draw the read-only look on a value and a field alike.
