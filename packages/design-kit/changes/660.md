A read-only value of words wraps and shows whole at a large text size, written as a read-only
textarea.

- **Upgrading.** A read-only `InputField` is one line and cuts off words that run past its edge, as
  "Round 3 of 10" does at 320 wide with twice the text size and the wider text spacing on some
  typefaces. A page that shows a value of words read only writes it as a read-only `TextareaField`,
  `<textarea class="dk-textarea" readonly>`, as the reference's example does. A read-only
  `InputField` stays for a short figure and for a field held read only for a moment while it saves.
- **Sheets.** A read-only `dk-textarea` is as tall as its value rather than its rows, with
  `field-sizing: content`, its block padding a field's, so one line stands at a field's height, and
  it has no resize handle.
- **Docs.** The reference says how a read-only value that may be long is written, and its example of
  read-only fields draws the round as a read-only textarea. The accessibility notes say why a
  read-only text field can be cut off at that size and what a screen reader calls the textarea.
- **Tests.** Browser tests hold every read-only value's width and height at 320 wide with twice the
  text size and wider spacing, with a value longer than its field on any machine's typefaces.
