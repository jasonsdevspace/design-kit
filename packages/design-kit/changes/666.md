An attached stepper is the one owner of its error, so a field shows one error at a time, its mark
goes back to "false" as the error clears, and a reset that takes a stepper's error away is heard.

- **Upgrading.** A required stepper submitted blank, or holding letters, shows its own range error
  at a submit, where the form drew "Enter a value." or "Enter a number." above it. Such a stepper is
  left out of the first list `invalid` is handed, and `shown`, the second, lists it with its range
  error's words. A page's `errorWords` and a field's `data-error-*` do not reach an attached stepper
  with a range error, so a page that gave words for a stepper's failure there gives them as
  `rangeError` on `StepperField`, or in the range error's markup, written as an instruction, since
  it is shown for a blank required field and for letters as well as a value past the limits or
  between steps.
- **Controls.** `attachForm` has each attached stepper that fails a submit show its own error, for a
  blank or letters as for a value past its range, and never draws an error on the stepper nor marks
  it, so a value typed past the range after a refused submit shows one error and one description,
  where it showed both, and an error the form drew on a stepper for a page's own refusal goes once
  the stepper's own error shows. A stepper whose error clears sets `aria-invalid="false"` where the
  kit checks its form, as the form marks every field not in error, where it took the mark away and
  could leave a stepper the form had marked first at "true" with no error shown. A reset that takes
  away an error the kit drew, or leaves fewer fields marked invalid than it found, fields tied to
  the form from outside among them, says "Form cleared.", whoever marked them, so a form refused
  through `useFormRefusal` is heard as its stepper's error clears, where only errors the kit drew
  were counted. A stepper clears its error on a reset before its form counts, and keeps it through a
  reset the page calls off.
- **React.** `useFormRefusal` hands `invalid` nothing for a stepper showing its own error, a blank
  or letters among its failures, and says "Form cleared." on a reset that takes an error away.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper and refused form sections, and records 20 and 21, say an
  attached stepper owns its error at a submit and a reset, how its mark clears, and when a reset
  says the form is cleared.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, in a form attached with `attachForm` and
  one refused through `useFormRefusal`, submit a required stepper blank and find its own error alone
  with the page handed nothing for it, then type a value past the range and find one error drawn and
  one described, find `aria-invalid="false"` once the error clears by a value put right, after a
  submit and by a reset, find no mark on a stepper outside any checked form, and hear "Form
  cleared." once on a reset that takes a stepper's error away and nothing on one with none showing.
  They keep a stepper's error through a reset the page calls off, take away the form's error on a
  stepper once its own shows, and hear "Form cleared." for a field tied to the form from outside and
  for one the page marked invalid.
