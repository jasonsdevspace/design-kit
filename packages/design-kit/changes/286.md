An icon takes one class.

- **Sheets.** `dk-icon` draws an inline svg one em square, stroked in the text's color at
  `max(1.5px, 0.125em)` that does not scale with the drawing, with round caps and joins and no fill,
  a path drawn in fill with no outline, and a tab bar's icons at their own size with the stroke that
  size takes.
- **Docs.** The reference shows an icon's markup, its attributes and its words, and warns against an
  svg `title`.
- **Tests.** An icon is its line's font size at the usual and a doubled size, stroked in its line's
  color at a weight that holds in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, in the system's text and link colors
  in forced colors, and a control whose face is an icon alone is named by its words.
