A status line taken off the page says nothing more and keeps no timers.

- **Controls.** Words waiting on a status line that has been taken off the page, said at once or settling, are dropped as they come due rather than put in, and the line sets no new timer, so a `StatusProvider` unmounted a moment after its parts spoke leaves nothing running. A test that unmounts a screen no longer finds a timer from the kit outliving its page.
- **Tests.** A browser test says words at once and words that settle on a line, takes it off the page before they are put in, and finds nothing put in, nothing heard and no error.
