A status line a page takes away can be let go of, so nothing more is said on it and nothing it set keeps running.

- **Controls.** `releaseStatus(line)` lets a status line go: it clears the line's timers, drops the words said or held for it and empties it, and the function `attachStatus` returned, and any piece speaking on the line, say nothing there and set no timer until the line is attached again. A line built before it is put on the page still speaks as before.
- **React.** `StatusProvider` lets its line go as it unmounts, so a screen taken away a moment after its parts spoke leaves no timer of the kit's running, as a test that unmounts a screen found one outliving its page.
- **Docs.** The reference's status line section names `releaseStatus`, and its list of what `./controls` exports names it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests say words at once, words that settle and words after the line is let go of, and hear nothing with no timer left to run, let a line go while words are on it with polite words waiting and hear nothing more until it is attached again, and hear a line built before it is on the page.
