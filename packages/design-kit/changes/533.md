A status line a page takes away can be let go of, so nothing more is said on it and nothing it set keeps running.

- **Controls.** `releaseStatus(line)` lets a status line go: it clears the line's timers, drops the words said or held for it and empties it, and the function `attachStatus` returned says nothing there and sets no timer until the line is attached again. A line built before it is put on the page still speaks as before. A busy button, a form's refusal and progress given a line that `releaseStatus` let go of do not attach it again on their next words, so the line stays silent for every piece, a copy button and a toast among them, until the page attaches it again with `attachStatus`, and letting a line go also clears the timer that ends the moment words were said in and forgets that firm words were said in it, so a line attached again at once takes polite words.
- **React.** `StatusProvider` lets its line go as it unmounts, so a screen taken away a moment after its parts spoke leaves no timer of the kit's running, as a test that unmounts a screen found one outliving its page. It calls off the moments it set to say words held before its line landed, if the line goes before they come.
- **Docs.** The reference's status line section names `releaseStatus`, and its list of what `./controls` exports names it. The reference and `releaseStatus`'s comment say a piece's own timers, such as a busy button's wait, come due saying nothing, and record 15 is amended for `releaseStatus`.
- **Tests.** Browser tests say words at once, words that settle and words after the line is let go of, and hear nothing with no timer left to run, let a line go while words are on it with polite words waiting and hear nothing more until it is attached again, and hear a line built before it is on the page. Another lets a line go under a busy button, finds its wait saying nothing, then attaches the line again and hears the busy words.
