A panel closed by a visitor's press inside it hands focus back, however the press closed it.

- **Controls.** The kit notes where a visitor's press lands while the page's own handlers for it
  run, so a panel closed from inside it, by its Close or by a Done button of the page's own that
  calls `set(false)`, moves focus to the button that opened it, though Safari and iOS take focus
  off what a pointer or a tap presses. A close from the page's code at any other time, such as on
  a timer, leaves focus where it is. The Close no longer needs a rule of its own for this.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close and the controls' doc comments say so.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel by a button of the page's own inside it, with the press
  leaving nothing focused as Safari does, and find focus handed back, and close it from the page's
  code a moment later and find focus left where it was.
