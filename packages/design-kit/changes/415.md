A panel that closes while nothing has focus hands focus back, however it closed.

- **Controls.** A panel that closes while focus is on the page's body, as a press of a button by
  pointer or touch leaves it in Safari and on iOS, moves focus to the button that opened it, so a
  Done button of the page's own that calls `set(false)` keeps the visitor's place too. Focus
  anywhere else outside the panel stays where it is. Attaching the first button of a panel its
  markup starts closed closes it without moving focus.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close and the controls' doc comments say so.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel from `set`, and by its Close, while nothing has focus and
  find focus handed back, and attach a button whose markup starts its shown panel closed and find
  focus not moved.
