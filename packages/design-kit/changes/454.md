A field can be marked required or optional in words in its label.

- **Sheets.** `.dk-field-mark` draws the mark in the muted text at the normal weight.
- **React.** The field, select, textarea, stepper and set of choices take `mark`, `'required'` or `'optional'`, apart from the native `required`, and `markWords` for a page's own words. A required field's mark is hidden from a screen reader and the control carries `aria-required`, an optional mark stays in the label's name, a set of choices keeps its words in its legend's name, and a set of radios carries `aria-required` as well. An `aria-required` the page gives a field with no mark is kept.
- **Docs.** The reference's Fields section shows a required and an optional field, drawn from a new example.
- **Tests.** Unit tests hold the marks' markup, and browser tests find a required field named without its words and marked required, an optional field named with them, every kind of field marked, sets of choices saying it in their legends with only radios marked, a page's own `aria-required` and words kept, the mark muted at the normal weight with no audit findings in both themes, drawn apart from the ground in forced colors, and whole at 320px with twice the text size and under wider text spacing.
