A field can be marked required or optional in words in its label.

- **Sheets.** `.dk-field-mark` draws the mark in the muted text at the normal weight.
- **React.** The field, select, textarea, stepper and set of choices take `mark`, `'required'` or `'optional'`, apart from the native `required`. A required mark's words are hidden from a screen reader and the control, or a set of radios, carries `aria-required`, an optional mark's words stay in the label's name, and a required set of checkboxes, which cannot carry `aria-required`, says it in its legend's words.
- **Docs.** The reference's Fields section shows a required and an optional field, drawn from a new example.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find a required field named without its words and marked required, an optional field named with them, every kind of field marked, a set of checkboxes saying it in its legend, the mark muted at the normal weight with no audit findings in both themes, shown in forced colors, and whole at 320px with twice the text size and under wider text spacing.
