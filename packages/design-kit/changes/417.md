A link in a panel that closes it leaves focus to where the link goes.

- **Controls.** A panel closed by a press on a link inside it, by pointer, touch or key, leaves
  focus to the link rather than handing it to the panel's button, since following a link moves
  focus of its own, so a panel of links on a phone puts the visitor at the section they chose.
- **Docs.** The reference says a link in the panel that closes it leaves focus to the link, and
  that a page closes a panel from a press's `click`, since a close from its `pointerdown` comes
  before the browser moves focus for the press.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel by a link inside it and find focus left off the panel's
  button, and tap the Close on a touch screen in WebKit and find focus on the panel's button.
