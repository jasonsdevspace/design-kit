A link in a panel that closes it keeps the visitor's place whether or not it moves focus.

- **Docs.** The reference says a link in the panel that moves focus to what it shows as it closes
  the panel keeps it there, and one that moves none, as many routers' links do, hands it back to
  the panel's button. It also says a page closes a panel from a press's `click`, since a close
  from its `pointerdown` comes before the browser moves focus for the press.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel by a link inside it that moves focus to a heading, and
  find focus there, and by one that moves none, and find focus on the panel's button, and tap the
  Close on a touch screen in WebKit and find focus on the panel's button.
