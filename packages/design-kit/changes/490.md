A view's heading takes focus on its first draw only when a visitor's recent press on a button or a link, or a submit, brought the view.

- **Controls.** `focusOnArrival(heading, { from })` moves focus to a view's heading as it is first drawn, as `moveFocus` does, only where the visitor's last input was a press on a button or a link, or a submit, in the last five seconds that no view has taken focus for. A page's first draw, a key that moves or types, such as Tab or an arrow along a row of tabs, a press on nothing, on a control that is off or in a row of tabs take nothing, and Enter on a page's own control that is a button or a link by its role counts, as Space does but for a link. A script's click counts as a press. Views first drawn for one press land together and the first in the page takes focus, and where none has a heading to take it, the first goes by its fallbacks. The module now notes inputs with one listener on the document as it loads in a page, so the press that brings a page's first view is heard, and a request called off before it lands hands its press back.
- **React.** `useArrivalFocus` decides a first draw through `focusOnArrival`, so it no longer takes focus from a row of tabs, after the visitor only tabbed or typed, or long after a press, and adds no listener as it draws again. A key that changes still moves focus at once. It calls its request off as its view leaves, and React's second run in development still counts as a first draw.
- **Docs.** The reference says a first draw takes focus for a recent press, as a tab bar's view does, that a row of tabs' panels leave the hook out, and names `focusOnArrival` for a page without React. Record 20 says the hook keeps only its key and whether it was a first draw, and leaves the decision to `./controls`. Record 13 sets the controls' budget at 13.75 KB by its rule.
