A refused form checks the browser's constraints itself, and draws the kit's errors in place of the browser's bubble.

- **Controls.** `attachForm` sets `noValidate` on a form it attaches, or on the form around an attached part of one, so a page without script keeps the browser's check. On a submit it reads the `validity` of each of the form's fields, the whole form even where a part is attached and fields tied to it by `form` among them, passing over a field marked off, counting a set once and a group of radios with no set once too. Where any fails it stops the submit, draws each error as the kit's field draws one, after its hint and marked as its set of choices marks one, and refuses, or hands the failures to `invalid`. An error the page already drew for a field takes the kit's words until the field passes, and a stepper's range error is left as the one error. Errors the kit drew go once their fields pass on a later checked submit, or the form is reset, with what they changed put back. A button marked `formnovalidate` submits unchecked and clears the kit's errors, and a reset the page calls off keeps them. A form's own attach takes over the check from a part attached first. Each failure takes the field's own words, as `data-error-value-missing` on the control or its set, else the page's `errorWords`, else the kit's, which say what to do. Fields not in error are marked `aria-invalid="false"` as the form is attached. A refusal passes over a control marked off, itself or by its set, and where no field in error can take focus it leaves focus where it is and names the first field with its error. New types `Failure`, `FailureWords` and `FieldFailure` describe the failures.
- **React.** `useFormRefusal` asks for `invalid`, which sets the page's errors from the failures it is handed, and the page's own submit handler clears them, and takes `errorWords`, read as each failure is put in words, and refuses after the render that draws the failures it handed the page.
- **Docs.** Record 21 says why the kit checks the browser's constraints itself, with the research and the options weighed. The reference shows the check, the words and how a field, a set or a page replaces them, keys a React page's errors by each failure's field, and says a form is attached as it is drawn and why `required` stays.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox refuse a form that fails its constraints before the page's handler, draw errors after their hints, name a field no one can reach, let a field marked off pass, count a group of radios with no set and a box alone in a section each as one field, give the page's own error the kit's words and put it back, give fields with no id errors of their own, check the whole form from an attached part, clear errors on a reset, leave a stepper's range error alone, take each failure's words in turn, and hand a React page the failures of a field and a set. Others refuse a form written without `noValidate` and sets marked off.
