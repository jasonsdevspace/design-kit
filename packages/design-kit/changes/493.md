A refused form shows the kit's errors whatever the page wrote, and focuses a field that can take focus.

- **Controls.** `attachForm` sets `noValidate` on a form it attaches, so the browser's own check no longer stops a submit before the page's handler runs and shows its passing bubble in place of the kit's errors. Its fields keep `required`, which a screen reader says. A refusal passes over a control marked off, as a button off in a set of choices, rather than choosing one focus cannot go to, and the form and a group of cells share one check of what can take focus.
- **Docs.** The reference says an attached form takes `noValidate` and why, that `required` stays for a screen reader, and that a refusal passes over a control marked off. The field's `mark` comment says the same.
- **Tests.** Browser tests refuse a form the page wrote with a required field and no `noValidate`, and a set in error whose first control is marked off.
