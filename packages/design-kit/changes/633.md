A page's function that throws as the status line reads its words leaves the line free.

- **Controls.** A message given as a function that throws as it is put on a status line, such as a
  stepper's `words`, is left unsaid, the line is freed for the polite words that wait for it, and
  the error is reported through `reportError`. Before, the line stayed claimed by words never
  written, and every later polite message waited until firm words replaced them.
- **Docs.** The reference's section on the status line says what a function that throws leaves.
- **Tests.** A unit test says firm words that throw, then polite words after them and behind them,
  and finds the polite words said and the error reported.
