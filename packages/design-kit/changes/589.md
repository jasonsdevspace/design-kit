A toast keeps the control that has focus inside a frame of the page's own origin clear of itself as
a message shows, and leaves alone a control it does not cover.

- **Controls.** As a message shows with focus inside a frame of the page's own origin, the toast
  measures the control that has focus there, read as the message comes, where the frame places it on
  the page, past the frame's border and padding and scaled across and down as the frame is drawn,
  rather than the whole frame. Where the toast would cover that control, it scrolls the control into
  view through the frame and the page, by the room the sheet keeps under a shown toast, so a tall
  frame no longer hides it. A control there that the toast does not cover stays where it is, though
  the frame reaches under the toast, and so does one the frame has scrolled out of its own sight or
  behind its scrollbars. A frame of another origin is measured whole, as before. For a control on
  the page or in a frame, the toast is now measured where it comes to rest rather than where its
  entrance starts, so a control it covers by a few pixels is scrolled clear too, and a control
  counts as covered only where it meets the toast across as well as down, so one beside a narrow
  toast on a wide screen no longer scrolls.
- **Docs.** The toast's doc comment, the reference's section on the toast and record 23 say how the
  toast keeps the control inside a frame of the page's own origin clear of itself, and that it
  measures the toast at rest and across as well as down. Record 23 gives the controls' new weight,
  and record 13 adds the raise it takes.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find a button near the foot of a tall
  frame of the page's own origin, with a border and padding, focused where the toast draws, ending
  clear of the toast once a message shows, on a page and in an app's frame. They find kept clear a
  button the frame's padding puts a few pixels under the toast's edge, in a frame drawn smaller too,
  one in a frame drawn wider than laid out, a link on the page the toast covers by less than the
  rise it enters on, and one in a frame scaled by `zoom` in Chromium and Firefox. They find the page
  and the frame left where they are where the button inside a frame is clear of the toast though the
  frame reaches under it, where a short frame above the toast has scrolled its button out of sight,
  where WebKit lays the button of a frame scaled by `zoom` past what the frame shows, where a
  control inside a frame is taller than the room above the toast, and in WebKit where the button
  sits behind the frame's scrollbar, and the page left where it is where a control on the page or
  inside a frame sits beside a toast narrower than the screen.
