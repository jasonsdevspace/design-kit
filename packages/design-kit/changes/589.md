A toast keeps the control that has focus inside a frame of the page's own origin clear of itself as
a message shows.

- **Controls.** As a message shows with focus inside a frame of the page's own origin, the toast
  measures the control that has focus there, read as the message comes, where the frame places it
  on the page, rather than the whole frame. Where the toast would cover that control, it scrolls the
  control into view through the frame and the page, by the room the sheet keeps under a shown toast,
  so a tall frame no longer hides it. A control there that the toast leaves alone stays where it is,
  though the frame reaches under the toast. A frame of another origin is measured whole, as before.
- **Docs.** The toast's doc comment, the reference's section on the toast and record 23 say the
  toast keeps the control inside a frame of the page's own origin clear of itself, and record 23
  gives the controls' new weight.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find a button near the foot of a tall
  frame of the page's own origin, focused where the toast draws, ending clear of the toast once a
  message shows, on a page and in an app's frame, and the page left where it is where the button
  inside a frame is clear of the toast though the frame reaches under it.
