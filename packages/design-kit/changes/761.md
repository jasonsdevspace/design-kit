A view a press brings into a closed shadow root for the first time focuses its heading, where the kit has read focus in that root, and leaves focus where a visitor moved first.

- **Controls.** The kit hears the presses and keys inside each closed shadow root it has read focus in, by listeners on the root itself, and a press keeps the control it was made on. Before, a press inside a closed root reached the page as a press on its host, so the view it brought took no focus.
- **React.** `useArrivalFocus` reads where focus was through the control pressed as its view first draws, before its heading is there, so its heading takes focus, a visitor who moves on to another control in the root first is left there, and a field the view focuses as it draws keeps its focus. The first press in a widget the kit has not read focus in yet still brings no focus.
- **Docs.** Record 19 says so and names what was turned down, and the reference says so in its sections on where focus can be and focus on arrival.
- **Tests.** Browser tests bring a view by a press inside a closed shadow root in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit: its heading takes focus, a visitor who moves first is left there, a field the view focuses above or below its heading keeps focus, a Tab or a click on nothing after the press leaves focus where it is, and a widget the kit was never handed a node of takes no focus for its first press and takes it from the next.
