A focus request a long render holds past a second on a page in sight lands, and one is dropped
where the page went out of sight before its frame, or where its frame comes more than ten seconds
late.

- **Upgrading.** A focus request from `moveFocus` or `focusOnArrival` that a long render holds past
  a second lands, where it was dropped. A page that relied on the drop to leave focus alone after a
  slow render calls the request off instead.
- **Controls.** `moveFocus` and `focusOnArrival` drop a request made while the page is out of sight,
  or before it goes, as a listener the module adds for the page going out of sight or coming back
  tells, and one whose frame comes more than ten seconds late, as a frame the browser holds back out
  of view can, rather than any request still waiting a second later. A view asked of
  `focusOnArrival` once the page is back in sight lands without the views asked before it went.
- **Docs.** The reference says which request is dropped, and record 13 names the listener.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox hold the thread past a second after a
  request on a page in sight and find it landed, find a request dropped where it was asked out of
  sight, where the page went out of sight before its frame, told of its return or not, where its
  frame runs out of sight and where its frame comes more than ten seconds late, and find a view
  landed that joins views asked before the page went out of sight and came back.
