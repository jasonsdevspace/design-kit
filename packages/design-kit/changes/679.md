A focus request a long render holds past a second on a page in sight lands, and one made while the
page is out of sight, or before it goes, is dropped.

- **Upgrading.** A focus request from `moveFocus` or `focusOnArrival` that a long render holds past
  a second lands, where it was dropped. A page that relied on the drop to leave focus alone after a
  slow render calls the request off instead.
- **Controls.** `moveFocus` and `focusOnArrival` drop a request only where the page was out of sight
  as it was asked or went out of sight before its frame, as a listener the module adds for the page
  coming back into sight tells, rather than any request still waiting a second later.
- **Docs.** The reference says which request is dropped, and record 13 names the listener.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox hold the thread past a second after a
  request on a page in sight and find it landed, and find a request dropped where it was asked out
  of sight, or where the page went out of sight and came back before its frame.
