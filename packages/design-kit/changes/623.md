A row of fields sets fields side by side where they fit and each on its own line where they do not.

- **Sheets.** `dk-fields` is a row of fields that wraps. Each field but a stepper's asks for
  `--dk-fields-least`, 7rem until a page sets it, and shares what is left, and a stepper's field
  keeps its stepper's width. The fields stand `--dk-space-4` apart both ways and line up along their
  foot, so their controls stand level where one label takes two lines. At 320 and 390 wide a
  stepper's field and a text field sit side by side at the usual text size and each on its own line
  at twice it, every label whole. A field in the row holds no measure of its own, and in a form
  with no class attribute the row stands `--dk-space-4` from the fields around it.
- **Docs.** The reference documents the row and `--dk-fields-least`, with its markup and the React
  a page writes around the fields, and lists the property among those a page sets. The field and
  stepper sections point a page that sets fields side by side to the row, and keep a sentence for a
  page that writes its own. The accessibility notes say that in Firefox 120, which lacks `:has()`,
  a stepper's field in the row asks for a text field's least width, so its raise button can run
  past the field at a phone's width.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox draws a stepper's field beside a text
  field in a row at 320 and 390 wide, at the usual text size and at twice it, and finds them side
  by side with their controls lined up and then each on its own line a step apart, every label on
  one line, every control inside its field and no sideways scroll. It finds a stepper's control
  level with a text field's whose label takes two lines, two text fields side by side only where
  twice the least width and the gap fit, by default and with `--dk-fields-least` set, and a row in
  a plain form a step from the fields above and below it, its fields holding no measure.
