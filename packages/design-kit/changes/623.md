A row of fields sets fields side by side where they fit and each on its own line where they do not.

- **Sheets.** `dk-fields` is a row of fields that wraps. Every field in it starts from
  `--dk-fields-least`, 10rem until a page sets it, a stepper's width rounded up, and shares what is
  left, so no label, hint or error moves the field beside it. Two fields share a line where the row
  is at least 21rem wide, two lengths and the gap, which in a page's column is a screen from 368
  pixels, and a narrower row or a larger text size stacks them, every label whole. The fields stand
  `--dk-space-4` apart both ways and line up along their foot, so their controls stand level where
  one label takes two lines or one field shows a hint or an error. An inline stepper and a choice
  field each take a line of their own. The row holds to `--dk-field-measure` as a field does, a
  field in it narrows by the same property, and in a form with no class attribute the row stands
  `--dk-space-4` from the fields around it.
- **Docs.** The reference documents the row and `--dk-fields-least`, with its markup and the React
  a page writes around the fields, lists the property among those a page sets, and names the row
  under `--dk-field-measure`. It gives the length that holds a wider stepper at its full size,
  10.5rem for three figures, 12rem for a tall stepper and 13.25rem for a tall one of three, and
  asks a page that hides every field in a row to hide the row itself. The field and stepper
  sections point a page that sets fields side by side to the row, and keep a sentence for a page
  that writes its own. Record 25 gives why each field starts from a length rather than its content,
  with the options turned down, and record 24 counts the row among a plain form's spaced blocks.
  The accessibility notes say how far a stepper wider than 10rem runs past its field without
  `:has()` on a screen from 368 pixels, where a page leaves the length as it is.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox finds a stepper's field and a text field
  at equal widths a step apart with their controls level at 390 and 1280 wide, and each on its own
  line at 320 wide and at twice the text size, every label on one line, every part inside its field
  and no sideways scroll. It finds a stepper no wider than 10rem, the same boxes drawn without
  `:has()`, neither field moved by a range error or a long label, a long address breaking inside its
  field, an inline stepper and choices each on a line of their own, two text fields sharing a line
  only where twice the length and the gap fit, three at 1280, both controls level beside a hint,
  and a row in a plain form a step from the fields around it, ending at their edge, with a field in
  it narrowed by `--dk-field-measure`. It finds a tall stepper, a tall one of three figures and one
  of three, at the lengths the reference gives, inside their fields with their buttons at full size
  beside a text field, with and without `:has()`. A sheet test fails a rule that names the row with
  anything after it unless it only sets how a field the row holds flexes, from a basis no content
  sets.
