A row of fields sets fields side by side where they fit and each on its own line where they do not.

- **Sheets.** `dk-fields` is a row of fields that wraps. Every field in it starts from
  `--dk-fields-least`, 10rem until a page sets it, a stepper's width rounded up, and shares what is
  left, so no label, hint or error moves the field beside it. Two fields share a line from a screen
  368 pixels wide, and a narrower screen or a larger text size stacks them, every label whole. The
  fields stand `--dk-space-4` apart both ways and line up along their foot, so their controls stand
  level where one label takes two lines or one field shows a hint or an error. An inline stepper
  and a choice field each take a line of their own. The row holds to `--dk-field-measure` as a field
  does, a field in it narrows by the same property, and in a form with no class attribute the row
  stands `--dk-space-4` from the fields around it.
- **Docs.** The reference documents the row and `--dk-fields-least`, with its markup and the React
  a page writes around the fields, lists the property among those a page sets, and names the row
  under `--dk-field-measure`. The field and stepper sections point a page that sets fields side by
  side to the row, and keep a sentence for a page that writes its own. Record 25 gives why each
  field starts from a length rather than its content, with the options turned down, and record 24
  counts the row among a plain form's spaced blocks. The accessibility notes say that without
  `:has()` a stepper of three figures at the usual length runs a few pixels into the gap on a screen
  368 to 381 pixels wide.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox finds a stepper's field and a text field
  at equal widths a step apart with their controls level at 390 and 1280 wide, and each on its own
  line at 320 wide and at twice the text size, every label on one line, every part inside its field
  and no sideways scroll. It finds a stepper no wider than 10rem, the same boxes drawn without
  `:has()`, neither field moved by a range error or a long label, a long address breaking inside its
  field, an inline stepper and choices each on a line of their own, two text fields sharing a line
  only where twice the length and the gap fit, three at 1280, both controls level beside a hint,
  and a row in a plain form a step from the fields around it, ending at their edge, with a field in
  it narrowed by `--dk-field-measure`. A sheet test fails a rule for what the row holds that sets
  anything but how a field flexes, takes its basis from content or reaches inside a field.
