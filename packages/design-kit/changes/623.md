A row of fields sets fields side by side where they fit and each on its own line where they do not.

- **Sheets.** `dk-fields` is a row of fields that wraps. Each field but a stepper's asks for
  `--dk-fields-least`, 7rem until a page sets it, and shares what is left, and a stepper's field
  keeps its stepper's width, its label, hint and error wrapping inside it, so a long label or an
  error shown never moves the field beside it. An inline stepper and a column of choices each take
  a line of their own. The fields stand `--dk-space-4` apart both ways and line up along their
  foot, so their controls stand level where one label takes two lines or one field shows a hint or
  an error. At 320 and 390 wide a stepper's field and a text field sit side by side at the usual
  text size and each on its own line at twice it, every label whole. The row holds to
  `--dk-field-measure` as a field does, its fields sharing that width, and in a form with no class
  attribute it stands `--dk-space-4` from the fields around it. Its fields take their least width
  from their parts by rules that need no `:has()`, so in Firefox 120 a stepper's field in the row
  still keeps its stepper's width.
- **Docs.** The reference documents the row and `--dk-fields-least`, with its markup and the React
  a page writes around the fields, lists the property among those a page sets, and names the row
  under `--dk-field-measure`. The field and stepper sections point a page that sets fields side by
  side to the row, and keep a sentence for a page that writes its own.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox draws a stepper's field beside a text
  field in a row at 320 and 390 wide, at the usual text size and at twice it, and finds them side
  by side with their controls lined up and then each on its own line a step apart, every label on
  one line, every control inside its field and no sideways scroll. It finds a stepper's field at
  its stepper's width with its range error shown and with a long label, the text field still
  beside it with the controls level, a stepper's control level with a text field's whose label
  takes two lines, an inline stepper with a hint and a column of choices each on a line of their
  own, two text fields side by side only where twice the least width and the gap fit, by default
  and with `--dk-fields-least` set, and a row in a plain form a step from the fields above and
  below it, ending where the field above it ends.
