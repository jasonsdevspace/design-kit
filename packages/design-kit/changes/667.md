A stepper shows its error for letters typed into a blank field as focus leaves it, and the words it
says of its own error name its field first.

- **Upgrading.** A test that reads a stepper's words as focus leaves a wrong value finds the field's
  name first, as "Bid: Error: Enter a whole number from 0 to 10.", where it found the error alone. A
  page whose `rangeError` already names the field drops the name from it, since the status line says
  the name before it. Words for a press, the value alone, stay as they were. A field's name the kit
  reads, in a stepper's words and in a refusal, leaves out what its label or legend hides from a
  screen reader, so a test that heard "Bid (required): Error: …" for a field with a required mark
  hears "Bid: Error: …".
- **Controls.** `attachStepper` shows its error, marks the field invalid and says the error once as
  focus leaves letters typed into a blank field, which leave the value blank and fire no `change`,
  where it showed nothing until a submit. A blank that focus only passes through stays quiet, as do
  letters in a field that is disabled or read only or shows an error of the page's, and focus that
  stays on the value as the window is switched. A press of the stepper's own button straight after
  the letters is left to the press, so no error is drawn above the field as the pointer goes down
  and the press lands. The words it says of its own error as focus leaves a wrong value start with
  the field's name, taken as a refused form takes it, without a mark the label hides, so a screen
  reader user whose focus has moved on hears which field the error is about. A refusal from
  `attachForm` names its field the same way, without what the label or legend hides from a screen
  reader, where it said a required mark as part of the name.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper section says when letters show their error and what the stepper
  says then, and its refused form section and record 21 say a field's name leaves out a mark its
  label hides, and record 21 says the same of the stepper under its rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox leave letters typed into a blank stepper
  by Tab and find its error drawn, the field marked invalid and the error said once, pass a blank
  required stepper by Tab and find nothing, press the stepper's own button straight after letters
  and find the press landed with no error drawn, find no error for letters as focus stays, as the
  field turns read only or beside the page's own error, leave a hidden required mark out of the name
  said by a stepper and by a refusal, and find the field's name before the error on the status line,
  with a press saying the value alone. In a form attached with `attachForm` and one refused through
  `useFormRefusal`, they hear the letters' error after the field's name as focus leaves, then the
  refusal alone, and submit right after the letters by Enter and by a press on the button and find
  one error and one message.
