A stepper shows its error for letters typed into a blank field as focus leaves it, and the words it
says of its own error name its field first.

- **Upgrading.** A test that reads a stepper's words as focus leaves a wrong value finds the field's
  name first, as "Bid: Error: Enter a whole number from 0 to 10.", where it found the error alone.
  A page whose `rangeError` already names the field drops the name from it, since the status line
  says the name before it. Words for a press, the value alone, stay as they were.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper` shows its error, marks the field invalid and says the error once as
  focus leaves letters typed into a blank field, which leave the value blank and fire no `change`,
  where it showed nothing until a submit. A blank that focus only passes through stays quiet. The
  words it says of its own error as focus leaves a wrong value start with the field's name, taken
  as a refused form takes it, so a screen reader user whose focus has moved on hears which field
  the error is about.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper section says when letters show their error and what the
  stepper says then, and record 21 says the same under the stepper rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox leave letters typed into a blank stepper
  by Tab and find its error drawn, the field marked invalid and the error said once, pass a blank
  required stepper by Tab and find nothing, and find the field's name before the error on the
  status line, with a press saying the value alone. In a form attached with `attachForm` and one
  refused through `useFormRefusal`, they hear the letters' error after the field's name as focus
  leaves, then the refusal alone, and submit right after the letters by Enter and by a press on the
  button and find one error and one message.
