Every attach tells of a missing part at its first attach, before it reads its signal.

- **Controls.** `attachStepper` and `attachPanel` throw for a missing part, a stepper's value or
  buttons and a panel button's `aria-controls`, under a signal already aborted too, as `attachToast`
  and `attachTable` do. Before, each returned a function that does nothing, so markup missing a
  part said nothing. A stepper, a panel's button or a toast already attached is handed back as it
  is by a repeat attach, though its markup has since lost a part, where a toast threw before.
- **Docs.** The reference's section on taking a control down, record 22 and record 13 say a missing
  part is told at the first attach, before the signal is read, and that an attached control is
  handed back as it is.
- **Tests.** A unit test calls every attach that throws for a missing part with markup missing it
  under a signal already aborted and finds each throw, and another finds every error the controls
  throw, of any kind, among those cases. Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox take a part
  from an attached stepper, panel's button and toast, and find a repeat attach returning the
  control and a fresh attach refused by name.
