A picture of an app's frame ends its body at what it holds.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-app-body` is the least height of an app's body, 8rem, a few lines it can scroll,
  and none inside an element with `dk-picture`, since a picture cannot scroll.
- **Sheets.** A frame's body keeps `--dk-app-body`, so a pictured frame's body ends at what it holds,
  where it kept 8rem and left an empty band under a few words. A pictured frame drawn taller than
  what it holds still gives the room to its body, its bars at its foot.
- **Docs.** The reference's tokens name `--dk-app-body`, and its app frame says the body keeps it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find a pictured frame's body ending one padding below its last child at
  390 and 1280 wide, and a pictured frame drawn taller than what it holds keeping its bars at its
  foot. A dialog that ends in a list or a paragraph stands the same room at its foot as at its top,
  at 390 and 1280 wide.
