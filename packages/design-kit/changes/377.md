A picture of an app's frame ends its body at what it holds.

- **Sheets.** A frame that carries `dk-picture` or sits inside an element with it draws each of its
  rows at what it holds, its body included, where its body kept the frame's least height of 8rem
  and left an empty band under a few words. A frame that is not a picture keeps that least height,
  so a screen's scrolling body still shows a few lines.
- **Tests.** A browser test finds a pictured frame's body ending one padding below its last child
  at 390 and 1280 wide, and one finds a dialog that ends in a list standing the same room at its
  foot as at its top.
