A narrow table fits a phone rather than cutting its last figure under the shade.

- **Sheets.** At 48rem wide or less a table's cells keep a step closer to each other, so a table of a word and four short figures fits a 320px phone at the usual text size.
- **Docs.** The reference says how narrow a table fits a 320px phone.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a record table of a word and four short figures at 320px wide, on a content page and in an app's frame, and finds its frame does not scroll.
