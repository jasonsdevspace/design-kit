A stepper attached with a value past its limits shows its error at once, with nothing said.

- **Controls.** `attachStepper` shows the stepper's error as it attaches when the value is outside the limits or between steps, describes the field by it and marks it invalid, and says nothing on the status line, so a page that draws that state, as the kit's site does, writes no code of its own for it. A field drawn blank shows no error until it is changed, and the error is cleared as before once the value is right, the form is reset or the stepper is taken away.
- **Docs.** The reference and the attach function's comment say a value drawn past the limits shows the error as the stepper is attached.
- **Tests.** A browser test attaches a stepper past its limit and finds the error shown, the field described by it and marked invalid, and its status line empty, then finds the error cleared by a right value, by taking the stepper away and by a form's reset, and finds a field attached blank with no error.
