A part of the page that is loading shows a line only once the wait is long enough to notice.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-wait`, 400ms, is how long a wait goes unremarked before a loading line shows.
- **Sheets.** A `.dk-loading` line inside a region marked `aria-busy="true"` stays out of sight until the wait outlasts `--dk-wait`, by a delay that stands under reduced motion and in forced colors, then shows in the muted text.
- **Docs.** The reference has a section on a part of the page that is loading, drawn from a new example: the page puts the line in with the mark, takes both away in one change, puts a danger callout in the line's place where the wait fails, and says the wait on the status line where it follows a visitor's own action. It names the token.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find the line out of sight during the wait and shown after it, with motion reduced or not and in forced colors, in the muted text with no audit findings in both themes, and whole under wider text spacing and at 320px with twice the text size.
