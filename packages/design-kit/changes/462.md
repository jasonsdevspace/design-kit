A part of the page that is loading shows a line only once the wait is long enough to notice.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-wait`, 400ms, is how long a wait goes unremarked before a loading line or a busy look shows.
- **Sheets.** A `.dk-loading` line inside a region marked `aria-busy="true"` stays out of sight until the wait outlasts `--dk-wait`, by a delay that stands under reduced motion, then shows in the muted text.
- **Docs.** The reference has a section on a part of the page that is loading, drawn from a new example, and names the token.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find the line out of sight during the wait and shown after it, with motion reduced or not, in forced colors, and whole under wider text spacing.
