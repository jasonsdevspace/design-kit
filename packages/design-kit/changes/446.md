An app's dock can hold a compact action beside its main one on one row.

- **Sheets.** `dk-app-dock-inline` lays out a compact action at its own width and the main action taking the rest of the row, never under the target or its own longest word, the two stretched to one height. Each button keeps the target as its least width with a step less side padding, a text button keeps its own look, a link drawn as a button centers its words, and two that cannot share a row take two rather than scroll the page sideways, with no page CSS. The tall button's comment names the row as the one place that brings its least width down.
- **Docs.** The reference's App frame section shows the dock row, drawn from a new example, says the main action goes last, nearest the thumb, and says how narrow the main action can be at twice the text size and when the pair takes two rows.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium and WebKit find Scores beside a main action on one row on a 320px phone, compact first, one height and at least the target, at twice the text size in Chromium too, two rows rather than a page scrolled sideways or a word cut for labels too long to share one, Scoreboard among them, every word whole under wider spacing, and no audit findings in both themes and in forced colors.
