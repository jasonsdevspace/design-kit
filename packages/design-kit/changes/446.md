An app's dock can hold a compact action beside its main one on one row.

- **Sheets.** `dk-app-dock-inline` lays out a compact action at its own width and the main action taking the rest of the row, the two stretched to one height, each keeping the target as its least width with a step less side padding, with no page CSS.
- **Docs.** The reference's App frame section shows the dock row, drawn from a new example, and says the main action goes last, nearest the thumb.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium and WebKit find the pair on one row at the target size on a 320px phone, with Scoreboard beside a main action of three words, and at twice the text size in Chromium, in both themes, in forced colors, under wider text spacing, with no audit findings.
