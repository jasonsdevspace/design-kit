A change inside one field of a plain form restyles that field rather than every block after it.

- **Upgrading.** An empty callout written in a plain form without `data-empty` counts as one of the
  form's blocks, so first in the form it puts a step above the field after it. A page marks an empty
  callout `data-empty`, as `Callout` does for a live callout with nothing to say yet.
- **Sheets.** The plain form's spacing rule tells a block out of sight by `hidden`, `data-empty` and
  `dk-visually-hidden` alone, with no `:has()` on the callout before the block it spaces, which
  Chromium answered by restyling every later block for a change inside any one of them.
- **Docs.** The reference says a plain form counts an empty callout written without its mark as a
  block. Record 24 gives the reason and the cost it removes, and record 27 says its rule's last
  `:has()` of that shape is gone.
- **Tests.** The restyle bench shows a stepper's button in a plain form and holds the elements
  restyled to a tenth of the form. Browser tests stand a field a step below an empty callout written
  without its mark and none below a marked one, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox.
