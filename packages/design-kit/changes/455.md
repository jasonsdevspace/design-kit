- **Docs.** The reference's Callouts section shows the danger callout a page mounts empty with `role="alert"`, drawn from a new example, and how React fills it: Try again in the actions, so the alert reads the failure and never the button, the callout emptied as the load starts again, so the same failure is read a second time, and focus moved by `useFocusAfterDraw` to what was retried and back to the callout when the load fails again, while a failure the visitor did not ask for moves no focus.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium and WebKit mount the reference's products, find the callout empty and out of sight, the first failure filled with no focus moved, no button words in the alert, the same failure heard twice with focus on the heading and then the callout, and the callout emptied and marked again when the load works. The filled failure stays whole on a 320px phone at twice the text size in Chromium and under wider spacing, with no audit findings in both themes and in forced colors.
