A dated field's range error gives its limit in words a visitor reads and names the kind of value.

- **Controls.** `attachForm`'s words for a date, a date and time, a time, a month or a week before
  its least or past its most give the limit in the page's `lang`, or the browser's language where
  the page names none, read as the time it holds with no zone shifting it, and name the kind of
  value after it, as "Enter November 22, 2026 at 9:00 AM or a later date and time.", "Enter November
  2026 or a later month." and "Enter the week of November 23, 2026 or an earlier week.", counted
  from the week's Monday. No raw value such as "2026-11-22T09:00" or "2026-W48" is shown, and no
  stop sits against the limit.
- **Docs.** The reference's failure words give a date and time's range error and how each kind of
  dated limit reads.
- **Tests.** A browser test submits a date, a date and time, a time, a month and a week field below
  and above their limits and finds each error naming the kind of value, its limit in words with no
  stop against it, and a page in British English given its own order, in Chromium, WebKit and
  Firefox, where WebKit and Firefox draw a month and a week as text fields with no limits.
