A dated field's range error gives its limit in words a visitor reads, ending on "or later" or "or
earlier".

- **Controls.** `attachForm`'s words for a date, a date and time, a time, a month or a week before
  its least or past its most give the limit as a visitor reads it and end on "or later" or "or
  earlier", as "Enter November 22, 2026 at 9:00 AM or later.", "Enter November 2026 or later." and
  "Enter the week of November 23, 2026 or later.", counted from the week's Monday. No raw value such
  as "2026-11-22T09:00" or "2026-W48" is shown, and no period sits against the limit. The limit is
  in the nearest `lang` around the field where that language is English, and in US English
  otherwise, since the words around it are English. It is the wall time written, so a limit in a
  spring-forward gap keeps its hour, a year under 100 stays as written, and a limit past any date a
  script can hold is shown as written. A date and its time are joined by "at" in every engine. A
  time whose least comes after its most is a window overnight, and a time in its gap takes "Enter a
  time between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM the next day."
- **Docs.** The reference's failure words give a date and time's range error, how each kind of
  dated limit reads and the words for an overnight window.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox submit a date, a date and time, a time, a
  month and a week field below and above their limits and find each error ending on "or later" or
  "or earlier" with its limit in words and no period against it, a page in British English given
  its own order, a page in French given US English with an English part of it given its own, a
  limit in a spring-forward gap in New York keeping its hour, a year under 100 kept, a limit past
  any date shown as written, and an overnight window for a time. WebKit and Firefox draw a month and
  a week as text fields with no limits.
