A field in a page's own row keeps its control at its own height beside a taller field.

- **Upgrading.** A page that gives a field more height than its parts, by a height or a flex grow
  of its own, and wants the control to fill it, such as a textarea that fills a panel, sets
  `grid-template-rows` on the field with `1fr` for the control's row, as `auto 1fr` for a label and
  its control or `auto auto 1fr` with a hint. Until now the field's rows shared the spare room, so
  the control took only a part of it.
- **Sheets.** `.dk-field` sets `align-content: start`, so a field stretched to a taller row, as
  beside a field whose label wraps, keeps its label, hint, error and control at their own heights
  and leaves the spare room below them. Two text fields in a `1fr 1fr` row at 390 wide keep their
  inputs at 44px where the shorter one grew to about 55px, and a stepper's field in an `auto`
  column at 320 wide and twice the text size keeps its buttons at the target. An inline stepper in
  a taller cell keeps its label and stepper at the top, its label level with the labels beside it,
  where it sat lower before. A field drawn alone is drawn as before.
- **Docs.** The reference's field section says a field shorter than the tallest beside it keeps its
  control at its own height, with the spare room below it, that an inline stepper there keeps its
  label level with the labels beside it, and how a page makes a control fill a field it stretches.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox draws two text fields in a page's own
  `1fr 1fr` row at 390 wide, one label wrapping, a text field with a wrapping label beside an
  inline stepper in the same row, and a stepper's field in an `auto` column at 320 wide and twice
  the text size, and finds each input and each stepper's buttons and value at the height of the
  same field drawn alone, and the inline stepper's label level with the text field's.
