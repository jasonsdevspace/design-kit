A stack can space what it holds at a pair's or a heading's step, and drops a list's markers.

- **Sheets.** `dk-stack-pair` spaces a stack's pieces at `--dk-space-2`, for a label over its value or a header's lines, and `dk-stack-heading` at `--dk-space-3`, for a heading over what it heads, each by setting `--dk-flow-step` on what the stack holds, in `tokens.css`. A stack inside one takes its step unless it names its own, and a hidden piece adds no step, by the flow a stack already had. A stack on a `ul` or an `ol` drops the markers, the indent and the list's own room.
- **Docs.** The reference has a Stacks section, drawn from a new example of a round's scores, that names each step, says a stack on a list carries `role="list"` since Safari drops the role with the markers, and leaves the step between a page's sections to the page. The token table names the two steps.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium and WebKit find each step between the pieces, a plain stack at the step of what holds it, no step for a hidden or visually hidden piece, a list with no marker, indent or room and its role kept, the example whole on a 320px phone at twice the text size in Chromium and under wider spacing with the reader's room added to the step, and no audit findings in both themes and in forced colors.
