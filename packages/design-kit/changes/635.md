In Chrome a change to a page restyles what it touches, where every element or word added restyled
the whole document.

- **Upgrading.** A page that writes a shown toast into a live app's frame by hand, with
  `data-shown` and no `attachToast`, writes `data-toast-rows` on the frame's `dk-app-body` too, with
  the value of the toast's `data-rows` or with no value for two rows, since the body keeps room
  under the toast by that mark. A toast shown through `attachToast`, and a picture of a frame, need
  nothing. A dialog on a page with an app's frame, or one that ends in a tab bar, is written inside
  the frame or straight before or after it, as its sibling. One set apart, such as inside a wrapper
  of its own, is drawn as a content page's dialog from 48rem wide, with controls at the field's
  height, the wider inset and its actions at its end.
- **Sheets.** No rule writes a `:has()` on the root, the body, an app's frame or a page that ends
  in a tab bar unless it draws that element, since Chromium restyles everything inside an element
  whose `:has()` draws something else. The body is the subject of its own rule for the padding
  under a page's toast. An app's frame keeps the room under its toast by `data-toast-rows` on its
  body, and a pictured frame by the shown toast it holds. A dialog takes an app's sizes where it
  sits inside a frame or a page that ends in a tab bar, or beside one. On a page of about 2,300
  elements in Chromium 158, an element added restyles 10 elements in 0.14 ms where it restyled
  every one in 12 ms, and words said on a status line take 0.06 ms where they took 6 ms. Showing a
  toast restyles the whole page once where it did twice. The room in a frame, and a dialog inside
  or after a frame, hold in a browser with no `:has()`. A picture of a frame with a shown toast
  inside a live frame leaves the live frame's body as it is.
- **Controls.** `attachToast` writes `data-toast-rows` on the body of the app's frame its toast
  sits in while the toast floats over that body, with the rows it measured or with no value for a
  toast it could not measure, and takes it off where the toast takes its row above the dock, as the
  toast is dismissed or taken down, and from a body the toast has left. It writes each attribute
  only where its value changes.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on the app's frame, the toast and the dialog say what marks
  the body, what a page that writes a shown toast by hand writes, and where a dialog is written on
  an app's page. Record 27 gives the measurements, the rule and the options turned down, and
  records 13, 15 and 22 name the mark.
- **Tests.** A browser bench in Chromium reads the elements each recalculation restyled from the
  browser's trace on a content page, a frame and a page that ends in a tab bar, and holds an element
  added, words on a status line and a field marked in error to a twentieth of what a change to the
  root's font size restyles, and showing a toast to no more than one restyle of the whole page.
  Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find the frame's body marked while a toast floats
  over it and unmarked in its row above the dock, after each way of dismissing it, after a takedown
  and on a body the toast has left. A sheet test fails a `:has()` on an element that holds the page
  in a rule that draws something else, and holds every list of an app's places to one form.
