A field in a page, a band or a stack holds to the measure, as the prose beside it does.

- **Upgrading.** A field in a page, a band or a stack, in a form there too, and a callout in such a
  form, are now no wider than `--dk-measure`. A page that wants one to run its column's width sets
  `--dk-field-measure: none` on the field or on its form, and a row of the page's own that sets
  fields side by side sets it on the row.
- **Sheets.** A field anywhere in a page, a band or a stack, and a callout in a form there, take
  `--dk-field-measure` as their widest, `--dk-measure` until a page sets it, so a field for a short
  name never runs the width of a wide panel and a form's callout ends where its fields end. A page
  narrows one with a shorter length, for a short answer such as a postcode, or widens one with
  `none`. A field in a dialog or a popover keeps the width the dialog or popover gives it, which a
  page may widen.
- **Docs.** The reference's fields say a field holds to the measure, how a page narrows or widens
  one and that a dialog or a popover sizes its own fields, its content page counts fields among
  what holds to the measure, and its table of properties a page sets lists `--dk-field-measure`.
- **Tests.** A browser test finds a field in a wide stack, a page and a band at 1280 no wider than
  the measure, a form's and one with a button beside its control too, a callout in the form at the
  measure and ending where its fields end, a callout outside a form at the column's width, a field
  a page widens at the stack's width, and a field in a kit dialog, a native dialog and a popover a
  page widens at their width.
