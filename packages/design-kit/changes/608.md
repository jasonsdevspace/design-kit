A field in a page, a band or a stack holds to the measure, as the prose beside it does.

- **Upgrading.** A field in a page, a band or a stack, in a form there too, is now no wider than
  `--dk-measure`. A page that wants one to run its column's width sets `--dk-field-measure: none`
  on the field or on its form.
- **Sheets.** A field anywhere in a page, a band or a stack takes `--dk-field-measure` as its
  widest, `--dk-measure` until a page sets it, so a field for a short name never runs the width of
  a wide card. A field in a dialog keeps the dialog's width, which a page may widen.
- **Docs.** The reference's fields say a field holds to the measure and how a page widens one, its
  content page counts fields among what holds to the measure, and its table of properties a page
  sets lists `--dk-field-measure`.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium and WebKit finds a field in a wide stack, a page and a band
  at 1280 no wider than the measure, a form's and one with a button beside its control too, a field
  a page widens at the stack's width, and a field in a dialog a page widens at the dialog's width.
