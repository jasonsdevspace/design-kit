A quiet Close has a place in the top corner of a dialog and a panel.

- **Sheets.** `dk-close` right after a dialog's or a panel's title places a quiet Close in the top
  corner, centered on the title's first line, without laying out what else the dialog or panel
  holds, so an eyebrow stays above the title and a button keeps its own width. What comes before
  the Close keeps free the room it takes, so the title wraps beside it, and the title is never
  shorter than keeps the Close's ring clear of what follows. The Close is the target at every width
  and text size, and a dialog's `dk-close` is a `form method="dialog"`, so it closes the dialog with
  no script. `attachDialog` moves focus off a corner Close as the dialog opens, to the first answer
  or the title, where nothing in the dialog asks for it.
- **Docs.** The reference's dialog shows a Close in the corner of a dialog and a panel: it comes
  after the title, never takes focus as a dialog opens, one Close to a dialog and none in one that
  must be answered, and a panel's names what it closes.
- **Tests.** Browser tests set a Close in the corner of a dialog and a panel at 320 and 1280 wide,
  at the default and twice the text size, and hold it to the target, clear of the title, which
  wraps, its ring inside the dialog, a dialog opening on its first answer and closing by the Close
  with no script, forced colors and the audit. The sheets' budget rises from 10.25 KB to 10.75 KB,
  set again by the size audit's rule with every change of 0.8.2 in place.
