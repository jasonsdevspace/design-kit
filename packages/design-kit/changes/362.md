A quiet Close has a place in the top corner of a dialog and a panel.

- **Sheets.** `dk-close` right after a dialog's or a panel's title sets a quiet Close in the top
  corner, the title wrapping beside it rather than under it. The Close is the target at every width
  and text size, held to the title's first line and its ring kept inside the dialog's padding, and a
  dialog's `dk-close` is a `form method="dialog"`, so it closes the dialog with no script.
- **Docs.** The reference's dialog shows a Close in the corner of a dialog and a panel: it comes
  after the title, never takes focus as a dialog opens, one Close to a dialog and none in one that
  must be answered, and a panel's names what it closes.
- **Tests.** Browser tests set a Close in the corner of a dialog and a panel at 320 and 1280 wide,
  at the default and twice the text size, and hold it to the target, clear of the title, which
  wraps, its ring inside the dialog, a dialog opening on its first answer and closing by the Close
  with no script, forced colors and the audit.
