Each job in the sheets is done one way, from the chosen state in forced colors to a card's inset.

- **Upgrading.** A toast's close is drawn by `dk-button-quiet` alone, so a toast written by hand
  gives its close that class beside `dk-toast-close`, as the reference's markup and the React
  `Toast` do. A link the strip or the footer names current by `aria-current="true"`, or any value
  but false, now stands in the text color and at the heading's weight, as one marked with `page`
  did.
- **Tokens.** `--dk-ground` and `--dk-ground-step` carry the ground of what holds a piece and the
  ground a piece steps to on it, set again by a dialog, a panel and a raised panel, so a piece takes
  the nearest container's however they nest. `--dk-card-inset` is how far a panel's and a tile's
  words stand in, and `--dk-app-inset` how far an app's column stands in from the page's sides.
- **Sheets.** One rule at the sheet's end draws every chosen thing in the selection's colors in
  forced colors. The footer clears a phone's foot unless a tab bar follows it, whatever else does. A
  tile's focus ring sits outside it, so a tile's ground holds its words and sign alone. A resting
  pill and tab take the text color under a press, and the site's brand underlines under a pointer
  and a press. A content page's lead stands the short step under its title, a tile's words start on
  a raised panel's line at every width, and a tile's title in an explainer takes the body size.
- **Tests.** Browser tests nest a dialog in a panel and a panel in a raised panel, follow the footer
  with a toast, a dialog and a script, press a pill, a tab and the brand where nothing hovers, mark
  the strip's link by each value of `aria-current`, and line a tile's words up with a raised panel's
  on a wide page, a phone and an app's frame. A token test holds the app's inset to one place.
