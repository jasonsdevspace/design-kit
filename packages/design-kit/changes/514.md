A hero, a group of sections and a split row written on a band, a content page or a stack keep their own spacing.

- **Sheets.** What a `dk-band-hero`, a `dk-sections` or a `dk-split` holds takes no step from the flow, by a rule after the flow's, since each spaces its pieces by its own gaps. A hero written on the same element as `dk-band` or `dk-page`, rather than inside one, no longer stands its picture the flow's step lower than its words, on a phone a step further from them and on a wider screen off center beside them, a group of sections written on a band no longer stands each section after the first a section's step lower, and a split row written on a stack keeps its ends on one line.
- **Docs.** The reference's band section says a hero can sit on an element inside a band, or on the band or the content page itself, and the sheet's comments on the flow and the hero say the same.
