A page refused through `useFormRefusal` or `attachForm` can build an error summary that lists a
stepper showing its own error, with the words the stepper shows.

- **Controls.** `attachForm` calls `invalid` with a second list, `invalid(failures, shown)`, which
  holds every failure in the form's order, each with its field, its control and the words its field
  shows: the form's words for a failure in the first list, and a stepper's own range error's words
  for one left out of it. The first list stays as it was, so a page that draws every failure it is
  handed never draws a second error on a stepper, and a page that takes one argument is unchanged.
- **React.** `useFormRefusal` hands its `invalid` the same second list.
- **Docs.** The reference's refused form section builds its summary from the second list, and
  record 21 says why the second list was chosen over marking the stepper's failure as drawn.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox refuse a React form with an empty
  required field and a stepper typed past its range, build a summary from the second list, and find
  the stepper listed with its own words and focus on the summary, and find a plain form attached
  with `invalid` handed both lists. The package's type check types the second list, with a page that
  takes one argument still compiling.
