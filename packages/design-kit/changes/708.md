A press or a key inside a frame of the page's own origin counts for a view's arrival as it does on
the page.

- **Controls.** The controls hear keys, clicks and submits on the document of each frame of the
  page's own origin, at any depth, as they load and as each frame loads a page, as well as on the
  page's. A press on a control inside such a frame brings focus to the heading of the view it opens
  through `focusOnArrival`, where it took none, and a key that moves or types inside one leaves no
  press made on the page standing, where a view that arrived after took focus from the frame's
  control the visitor had moved to. A frame of another origin and a frame inside a shadow root stay
  unheard.
- **React.** `useArrivalFocus` hears presses and keys inside such a frame as `focusOnArrival` does.
- **Docs.** The reference and record 19 say which documents' presses and keys a focus request
  hears.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find focus left in a frame where the visitor tabbed after a press on the
  page that brought a view, a view's heading taking focus as it arrives for a press inside a frame,
  a frame that loaded another page and a frame added inside a frame, and a frame's document heard
  once however often it is reached.
