A press or a key inside a frame of the page's own origin counts for a view's arrival as it does on
the page.

- **Controls.** The controls hear keys, clicks and submits on the document of each frame of the
  page's own origin, at any depth through such frames, as well as on the page's, an object showing
  such a page and a frame the page writes among them. They hear the frames they find as they load,
  and a frame's page as it loads, so a frame added later and the next page of a frame that goes on
  are heard from their load. A press on a control inside such a frame brings focus to the heading of
  the view it opens through `focusOnArrival`, where it took none, and a key that moves or types
  inside one leaves no press made on the page standing, where a view that arrived after took focus
  from the frame's control the visitor had moved to. A frame of another origin, a frame of the
  page's origin inside one and a frame inside a shadow root stay unheard. A keydown with no key, as
  autofill sends, counts for no input.
- **React.** `useArrivalFocus` hears presses and keys inside such a frame as `focusOnArrival` does.
- **Docs.** The reference and record 19 say which documents' presses and keys a focus request
  hears, what a page with a frame that is not heard does instead, and records 13 and 29 follow
  record 19.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find focus left in a frame where the visitor tabbed, or clicked a field
  and typed, after a press on the page that brought a view, and a view's heading taking focus as it
  arrives for a press inside a frame, a frame that loaded another page, a frame added inside a
  frame, a frame still loading, a frame the page wrote and an object. They find a keydown with no
  key throwing nothing and counting for nothing, and a frame's document heard once however often it
  is reached.
