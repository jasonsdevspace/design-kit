A grid of buttons and a row of tabs move by the arrow keys with one stop for the Tab key.

- **Controls.** `attachRoving(group, { orientation, current, onMove })` gives a group of cells one stop, the page's current cell, a tablist's chosen tab or the cell focus last reached, and moves focus by the arrows, Home and End: up and down to the nearest cell as drawn in a grid, left and right in reading order, a horizontal row ignoring up and down, and an arrow held with a modifier left to the browser. A cell marked off keeps its place, focus taken out with a cell goes to the cell in its place, a grid of buttons with no role takes the toolbar's, and the page chooses in `onMove`.
- **React.** `useRoving()` returns a ref that attaches a group, reading the page's `current` and `onMove` as the keys land.
- **Docs.** The reference's Tabs and Button grid sections show the helper in place of leaving the keys to the page, and say how a page chooses as focus moves. Record 14 notes the keys the kit now runs, and record 13 sets the controls' budget at 10.5 KB by its rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium and WebKit move about a grid as drawn and in a right to left page, keep a tab row's stop on the chosen tab and ignore up and down, let the page choose, reach a cell marked off, hand focus on from a cell taken out, let the group go, and keep one stop through the renders a choice brings in React.
