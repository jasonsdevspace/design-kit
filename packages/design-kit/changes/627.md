A stepper is wrong exactly where the browser's constraints say its field is, so its own error and
its form's refusal agree.

- **Upgrading.** A page that relied on a blank stepper showing its error without `required` adds
  `required` to the stepper, as `<StepperField required />` or on the input. A stepper left blank
  that is not required shows no error and its form saves.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper` reads the field's `validity` to decide whether its own error shows:
  a value outside the limits, between steps or that is no number, as letters typed, and none at all
  only in a field that is required, where a blank showed the error in any stepper while its form
  saved. A checked form keeps the fields its last submit refused and tells each attached stepper
  after every verdict and on a reset. A required stepper drawn blank and submitted untouched shows
  the stepper's own error, in a form attached with `attachForm` and one refused through
  `useFormRefusal`, where it took the form's "Enter a value." and a value typed past the range after
  it showed two errors. A stepper attached afresh in a refused form shows the error again, and one
  that passes on a later submit, is submitted from a button marked `formnovalidate` or is reset
  clears it. The error's words are the stepper's own, from `rangeError`, and it can show beside a
  page's own error between submits, once the value changes. The stepper follows `required` and
  `step` as a script changes them, as it does its limits.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper section says when a stepper is wrong, that one whose blank has
  no meaning takes `required`, and what a checked form shows on its stepper, and its lead stepper
  example is required. Record 21 says the stepper's own error follows the same constraints and reads
  the verdict the form keeps.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, in a form attached with `attachForm` and
  one refused through `useFormRefusal`, save a blank stepper that is not required with no error,
  refuse a required one left blank, one holding 2.5 and one holding a letter with the stepper's own
  error, and refuse a required stepper drawn blank and never touched with that error alone, a value
  typed past the range after it too. They find the error again on a stepper attached afresh, gone
  after a submit that passes, an unchecked submit and a reset, and gone as a script makes the field
  optional or changes its step.
