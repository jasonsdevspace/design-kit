A read-only stepper's value lines up with the read-only fields beside it.

- **Sheets.** A read-only `dk-stepper-value` shown alone starts its figures at a field's inline
  padding, where a read-only field's words start, and stands at a field's height at each width,
  unless the stepper is tall, where its figures start at the same place and it keeps the larger
  target. While a button keeps focus as the field turns read only, the value keeps the editable
  stepper's place. An editable stepper is unchanged.
- **Docs.** The reference says a read-only stepper keeps the edge of a column of read-only values,
  that it is read only for the page, and that a value beside a button holding focus keeps its place.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a read-only stepper beside a read-only text field and finds their
  values starting at the same place and the two standing the same height, at a phone's width and a
  laptop's, in an app's frame, and on a phone at twice the text size. Others find a tall read-only
  stepper at the larger target with its figures at a field's padding, and a value beside a button
  holding focus as its field turns read only keeping an editable stepper's width and place.
