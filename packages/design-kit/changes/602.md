A read-only stepper's value lines up with the read-only fields beside it.

- **Sheets.** A read-only `dk-stepper-value` shown alone starts its figures at a field's inline
  padding, where a read-only field's words start, and stands at a field's height at each width,
  unless the stepper is tall, where its figures start at the same place and it keeps the larger
  target. An inline stepper's read-only value keeps its centered figures at its row's end. While a
  button keeps focus as the field turns read only, the value keeps the editable stepper's place and
  the hidden button keeps its room out of sight, so nothing moves under the focused button. An
  editable stepper is unchanged.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper` hands focus to the value when it is taken down while one of its
  buttons has focus, so focus is never dropped to the page.
- **Docs.** The reference says a read-only stepper keeps the edge of a column of read-only values,
  that an inline one keeps its centered figures, that a stepper is read only for the page, that
  nothing moves under a button holding focus, and that a stepper taken down hands focus to its
  value.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a read-only stepper beside a read-only text field and finds their
  values starting at the same place and the two standing the same height, at a phone's width and a
  laptop's, in an app's frame, and on a phone at twice the text size, and an inline read-only value
  centered as an editable one is. Others find a tall read-only stepper at the larger target with its
  figures at a field's padding, the value and a focused button where they stood as the field turns
  read only, and focus on the value once a read-only stepper is taken down with a button focused.
