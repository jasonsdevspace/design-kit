A read-only stepper's value lines up with the read-only fields beside it.

- **Sheets.** A read-only `dk-stepper-value` shown alone starts its figures at a field's inline
  padding, where a read-only field's words start, and stands at a field's height at each width,
  unless the stepper is tall, where its figures start at the same place and it keeps the larger
  target. An inline stepper's read-only value keeps its centered figures at its row's end, as tall
  as one that can be changed. While a button keeps focus as the field turns read only, the value
  keeps the editable stepper's place and the hidden button keeps its room out of sight, so nothing
  moves under the focused button. Both rest on `:has()`, and without it a read-only value keeps its
  centered figures and every hidden piece stays hidden. An editable stepper is unchanged.
- **Docs.** The reference says a read-only stepper keeps the edge of a column of read-only values,
  that an inline one keeps its centered figures, that a stepper is read only for the page, and that
  nothing moves under a button holding focus.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a read-only stepper beside a read-only text field and finds their
  values starting at the same place and the two standing the same height, at a phone's width and a
  laptop's, in an app's frame, and on a phone at twice the text size, and an inline read-only value
  centered and as tall as an editable one. Others find a tall read-only stepper at the larger target
  with its figures at a field's padding, the value and a focused button where they stood as the
  field turns read only, and hidden pieces kept hidden with the sheets read as a browser without
  `:has()` reads them.
