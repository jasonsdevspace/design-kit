A date or a time field draws the kit's focus ring in Safari.

- **Sheets.** An input for a date, a time, a date and time, a month or a week draws the focus ring
  whenever it holds focus. Safari hands focus to a part inside such a field and never counts the
  field itself as focused by the keyboard, so there only its own highlight on the part showed, by
  Tab, by a press and after a refusal. Chromium and Firefox already show focus on these fields
  however it came, as on any field a visitor types in, so they draw the same one ring as before.
- **Docs.** The accessibility notes say a dated field draws the ring whenever it holds focus, and
  why.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox Tabs to each kind of dated field and
  finds the outline a text field draws and no other ring, and presses each and finds what a pressed
  text field draws.
