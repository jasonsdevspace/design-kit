A stepper taken down while one of its buttons has focus keeps focus off the page's body.

- **Sheets.** A `dk-stepper` that has focus shows the focus ring.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper`'s takedown, by its signal or its detach, hands focus from a focused
  button to the value where the value is read only, and otherwise to the stepper itself, which it
  gives `tabindex="-1"`, so an editable value opens no phone's keyboard and a disabled one is passed
  by. It does so before it hides the buttons and only where a button has focus, so focus the page
  moves in the abort's task stays and a `moveFocus` asked in that task still lands.
- **Docs.** The reference's section on taking a control down lists the stepper among the pieces
  that hand focus back, and its section on the stepper says where focus goes, the tabindex the
  stepper keeps and its ring. Record 23 says the same, why an editable value is skipped and what it
  costs.
- **Tests.** A browser test focuses a button of a read-only, an editable and a disabled stepper by
  the keyboard, takes each down by its signal and by its detach, and finds focus on the read-only
  value or on the stepper, never on the body or an editable value, both buttons hidden and the ring
  drawn, and focus kept there through the window losing focus and getting it back. Others find focus
  kept on the button where the page attaches the stepper again in the abort's task, focus the page
  moves in that task staying, and a focus request asked in that task landing.
