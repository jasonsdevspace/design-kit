A stepper taken down while one of its buttons has focus keeps focus off the page's body.

- **Upgrading.** A page that writes a stepper by hand gives its label an id and its box
  `role="group"` with `aria-labelledby` naming that id, so the box focus goes to has a name.
- **Sheets.** A `dk-stepper` draws the focus ring where the browser shows focus on it.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper`'s takedown, by its signal or its detach, hands focus from a focused
  button to the value where the value is read only and enabled, and otherwise, or where the value
  refuses focus, to the stepper's box, since an editable value would open a phone's keyboard and a
  disabled one refuses focus. A box with no tabindex is given one of -1 then, which stays, and a
  page's own tabindex is kept. Focus moves before the buttons hide and only where a button has
  focus, so focus the page moves in the abort's task stays and a `moveFocus` asked in that task
  still lands, and a page that attaches the stepper afresh as focus comes to it keeps its buttons.
- **React.** `Field`'s label, and so the label of every field the React components draw, has an id,
  the field's id followed by `-label`. `StepperField`'s box is a group named by it.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on taking a control down and on `moveFocus` list the stepper
  among the pieces that hand focus back, its section on the stepper says the box is a group named
  by the label, where focus goes and the tabindex the box is given, and every field's markup shows
  its label's id. Record 23 says the same, why an editable value is skipped and what it costs.
- **Tests.** A browser test focuses a button of a read-only, an editable and a disabled stepper by
  the keyboard, takes each down by its signal and by its detach, and finds focus on the read-only
  value or on the group named by the label, never on the body or an editable value, both buttons
  hidden and the ring drawn. Others find the box's attributes as drawn, but for the tabindex,
  through takedowns and attaches, a page's own attributes kept, older markup given a tabindex, a box
  that refuses focus given nothing more, the group's name kept as the value changes, a stepper
  attached again left out of the tab order, focus kept through the window losing focus and getting
  it back, the buttons kept where the page attaches the stepper afresh as focus comes to it, and
  focus the page moves or asks for in the abort's task landing. The leak test takes the stepper down
  with a button focused.
