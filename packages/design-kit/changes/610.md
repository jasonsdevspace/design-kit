A stepper taken down while one of its buttons has focus keeps focus off the page's body.

- **Upgrading.** A page that writes a stepper by hand gives its label an id, such as
  `id="players-label"`, and its `dk-stepper` box `role="group"` with `aria-labelledby` naming that
  id, so the box focus goes to has a name. A box without the role still takes focus, with no name.
  A page whose own element has the id of a React field followed by `-label` renames it, since
  `Field`'s label now takes that id.
- **Sheets.** A `dk-stepper` draws the focus ring where the browser shows focus on it.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper`'s takedown, by its signal or its detach, hands focus from a focused
  button to the value where the value is read only and enabled, and otherwise, or where the value
  refuses focus, to the stepper's box, since an editable value would open a phone's keyboard and a
  disabled one refuses focus. A box focus goes to with no tabindex is given one of -1, which stays,
  and a page's own tabindex is kept. Where the box refuses focus too, the tabindex it was given goes
  again at once, and focus goes on to the app frame's body or the page's main content. Focus moves
  before the buttons hide and only where a button has focus, so focus the page moves in the abort's
  task stays and a `moveFocus` asked in that task still lands, and a page that attaches the stepper
  afresh as focus comes to it keeps its buttons.
- **React.** `Field`'s label, and so the label of every field the React components draw, has an id,
  the field's id followed by `-label`. `StepperField`'s box is a group named by it.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on taking a control down and on `moveFocus` list the stepper
  among the pieces that hand focus back, its section on the stepper says the box is a group named
  by the label, where focus goes and the tabindex the box is given, and the markup of every field
  `Field` draws shows its label's id. Record 23 says the same, why an editable value is skipped and
  what it costs.
- **Tests.** A browser test focuses a button of a read-only, an editable and a disabled stepper by
  the keyboard, takes each down by its signal and by its detach, and finds focus on the read-only
  value or on the group named by the label, never on the body or an editable value, both buttons
  hidden and the ring drawn. Others find the box's attributes as drawn, but for the tabindex,
  through takedowns and attaches, a page's own attributes kept, older markup given a tabindex, a box
  that refuses focus left as drawn with focus sent on to the main content, the group's name kept as
  the value changes, a stepper attached again left out of the tab order, the buttons kept where the
  page attaches the stepper afresh as focus comes to it, and focus the page moves or asks for in the
  abort's task landing. The leak test takes the stepper down with a button focused.
