A stepper taken down while one of its buttons has focus keeps focus off the page's body.

- **Sheets.** A `dk-stepper` draws the focus ring where the browser shows focus on it.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper`'s takedown, by its signal or its detach, hands focus from a focused
  button to the value where the value is read only and enabled, and otherwise, or where the value
  refuses focus, to the stepper itself, since an editable value would open a phone's keyboard and a
  disabled one refuses focus. A stepper handed focus is given `tabindex="-1"`, and where its field
  has a label, the role of a group named by the label and the value, through the label's id where
  it has one and from its words otherwise. A tabindex, a role or a name the page gave the stepper is
  left as it is, and the stepper's next attach takes away what the kit gave. Focus moves before the
  buttons hide and only where a button has focus, so focus the page moves in the abort's task stays
  and a `moveFocus` asked in that task still lands, and a page that attaches the stepper afresh as
  focus comes to it keeps its buttons.
- **React.** `Field`'s label, and so the label of every field the React components draw, has an id,
  the field's id followed by `-label`, which names a stepper handed focus.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on taking a control down and on `moveFocus` list the stepper
  among the pieces that hand focus back, its section on the stepper says where focus goes, the
  tabindex, role and name the stepper is given and when they are taken away, and every field's
  markup shows its label's id. Record 23 says the same, why an editable value is skipped and what
  it costs.
- **Tests.** A browser test focuses a button of a read-only, an editable and a disabled stepper by
  the keyboard, takes each down by its signal and by its detach, and finds focus on the read-only
  value or on the stepper, a group named by its label and value, never on the body or an editable
  value, both buttons hidden and the ring drawn, and focus kept there through the window losing
  focus and getting it back. Others find a stepper named from a label with no id, focus going on to
  the stepper where a read-only value refuses focus, a page's own tabindex kept, what the kit gave
  taken away once the stepper is attached again, the buttons kept where the page attaches the
  stepper afresh as focus comes to it, focus kept on the button where the page attaches it again in
  the abort's task, focus the page moves in that task staying, and a focus request asked in that
  task landing.
