A wrapped row of fields stands its lines as far apart as the fields around it.

- **Sheets.** A `dk-fields` row written straight in a panel, a dialog, a stack or anything else that
  spaces what it holds by `--dk-flow-step` stands its lines that step apart, where it stood them
  `--dk-space-4` apart everywhere, wider than the step between the fields around it in a panel, a
  dialog or a stack inside one. A row in a plain form keeps the form's step. A text button's band is
  written from `--dk-leading-tight` rather than a length of its own, at the same size.
- **Docs.** The reference and record 25 say which step a wrapped row's lines stand apart. The
  comment at the head of `tokens.css` names every part of a browser's own element a component
  reaches, a progress bar's track and fill and the `::after` a busy button draws its dots in among
  them.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox wraps a row between two fields in a
  page, a panel, a dialog, a stack, a stack in a panel, a pair's stack, a plain form, a plain form
  in a panel and a stack nothing holds, and finds every step between the fields equal.
