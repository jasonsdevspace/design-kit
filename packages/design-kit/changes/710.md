The controls hear a frame of the page's own origin as focus enters it and its next page as it
begins, and clear a press where focus enters a frame they cannot hear.

- **Controls.** The controls find frames by a walk of the window tree, which reaches an object, a
  frame element and a frame of the page's origin held inside one of another origin. A frame is heard
  as focus enters it, before the click that focused it, a frame inside an open shadow root and one
  whose page is still loading among them, and a frame's next page is heard as it begins, before its
  load, so a press there brings focus to the view it opens where it brought none. Where focus enters
  a frame of another origin, a sandboxed one or one inside a closed shadow root after a press on the
  page, or a heard frame goes on to a page of another origin with focus inside, the press is
  cleared, so a view that arrives leaves focus in the field the visitor moved to inside it, where it
  took focus from that field.
- **React.** `useArrivalFocus` follows the same frames as `focusOnArrival` does.
- **Docs.** Record 19 says which frames are heard, what clears a press, and what stays unheard under
  the rule that every gap fails safe, with the reference and records 13 and 29 following it, and the
  reference points a page whose views arrive while focus is in a frame to the status line.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find focus left in a frame inside an open or a closed shadow root, a frame
  of another origin and a frame whose form went on to another origin after a press on the page, and
  a view's heading taking focus for a press in a frame added after its load while its page loads,
  in a frame's next page before its load and in an object in every engine. They find no page error
  as a frame is taken away while its next page is read, as a frame goes on to another origin, or as
  the page is left and gone back to.
