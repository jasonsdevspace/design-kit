The controls hear a frame of the page's own origin as focus enters it and its next page as it
begins, and clear a press where focus enters a frame they cannot hear.

- **Controls.** The controls find frames by a walk of the window tree, which reaches an object in
  every engine, a frame element and a frame of the page's origin held inside one of another origin.
  A frame is heard as focus enters it, before the click that focused it, a frame inside an open
  shadow root and one whose page is still loading among them, and a frame's next page of the page's
  origin is heard as it begins, before its load, so a press there brings focus to the view it opens
  where it brought none. Where focus enters a frame of another origin, a sandboxed one or one inside
  a closed shadow root from the page after a press there, or a heard frame goes on to a page of
  another origin with focus inside, the press is cleared, so a view that arrives leaves focus in the
  field the visitor moved to inside it, where it took focus from that field. A press in a frame
  inside a shadow root that was heard before stands, however quickly it follows focus in. Two gaps
  leave a press standing: a frame inside a closed shadow root entered from a heard frame, or from
  the page where a frame of the page's origin holds that root, and a frame being written with
  `open()`, which is heard only from its `close()`. In Firefox a page script that moves focus to the
  page just after focus enters a frame clears a press that should stand.
- **React.** `useArrivalFocus` follows the same frames as `focusOnArrival` does.
- **Docs.** Record 19 says which frames are heard, what clears a press, which gaps fail safe and
  which two do not, with the reference and records 13 and 29 following it, and the reference points
  a page whose views arrive while focus is in a frame to the status line.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find focus left in a frame inside an open or a closed shadow root, a
  frame of another origin and a frame whose form went on to another origin after a press on the
  page, and a view's heading taking focus for a press in a frame added after its load while its page
  loads, in a frame's next page before its load, in a frame inside a shadow root whether focus is
  read before its click or after it, and in an object, one loaded before the controls among them. A
  test in Chromium and WebKit finds a press standing through a mousedown on a frame's scrollbar.
  They find no page error as a frame is taken away while its next page is read, as a frame goes on
  to another origin, or as the page is left and gone back to.
