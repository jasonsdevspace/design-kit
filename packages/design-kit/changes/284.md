A row can hold a picture at its start, with tiles flowing into columns.

- **Upgrading.** A tile in a panel with no ground set draws on the page's ground in a plain panel,
  where it drew on the panel's own, and keeps the card's in a raised one.
- **Sheets.** `dk-row-picture` as a row's first child sits at its start in a column of its own,
  `--dk-target` square or the size a page sets by `--dk-row-picture-size` up to a quarter of the
  screen's width, so every title in a list starts on one line. `dk-rows-columns` beside
  `dk-rows-tiles` flows the tiles into as many columns as fit, each at least `--dk-tile-least` wide,
  15rem unless the page sets it, falling to one column on a phone, the tiles on one line ending
  together, with each title at the body size and a picture across the tile's top. A tile in a plain
  panel steps to the page's ground and one in a raised panel to the card's, where the page sets
  none.
- **Docs.** The reference shows a row with a picture and tiles in columns.
- **Tests.** A row and a tile with a picture start every title on one line with the ring clear of
  the picture and the title first in the link's name, tiles flow into columns on a wide page, ending
  together on each line, and into one on a phone at twice the text size and the wider spacing a
  visitor may set, in reading order for the keyboard, and a tile steps its ground in a panel with
  its words, sign and ring at their ratios.
