A toast the page attaches inside a frame of its own origin reads focus, and hears F8, in the frame's
document.

- **Controls.** `attachToast` on an element inside a frame of the page's own origin listens for F8
  and follows focus in that frame's document, so F8 pressed in the frame reaches the action and a
  dismiss hands focus back to the control in the frame, where it reached nothing and handed focus
  back to the frame. It listens to the frame's window for its size and scrolls the frame's window,
  not the page's, as it takes its row above the dock. A toast stays in the document it was attached
  in, so a page that moves it into another takes it down and attaches it again. Only the toast
  changes, and a toast on the page works as it did.
- **Docs.** The reference's toast section says a toast works inside a frame the page drives, and
  record 29 has an amendment for it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests attach a toast inside a frame from the page and find F8 reaching its
  action, a dismiss handing focus back to the control in the frame, one the page made and moved
  there among them, and the frame's listeners coming off at takedown.
