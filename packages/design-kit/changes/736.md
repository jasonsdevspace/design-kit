A dialog whose opener went with the page its frame left hands focus to that frame.

- **Controls.** A dialog keeps the frames its opener sits inside as it opens, and where the opener has gone and `returnTo` gives nothing that takes focus, hands focus to the innermost of them rather than leaving it on the page's body.
- **Docs.** The reference's dialog section and its section on where focus can be say so.
- **Tests.** The focus grid's dialog square for a frame that has loaded its next page finds focus on the frame in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
