A panel a button opens is attached by its button, so its corner Close needs no script of the
page's own.

- **Controls.** `attachPanel(button)` opens and closes the panel the button names in
  `aria-controls`, every attached button that names it following, closes it by its corner Close
  with focus back on the button that opened it, and returns a `SetPanel` that opens or closes it
  from the page's own code. One listener on the page serves every panel.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close attaches its panel with `attachPanel`, the README lists it
  and `SetPanel`, and the controls' budget is 7.25 KB, set by the budgets' rule with it in place.
- **Tests.** Browser tests open and close a panel by its button and its Close, by keyboard and
  pointer, with focus back on the button, keep two buttons in step, close a panel drawn again past
  a handler that stops the press, move focus out of a panel closed by the page's code, and attach a
  button twice to one function.
