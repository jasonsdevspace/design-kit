A panel a button opens is attached by its button, so its corner Close needs no script of the
page's own.

- **Controls.** `attachPanel(button)` opens and closes the panel the button names in
  `aria-controls`, closes it by its corner Close with focus back on the button, and returns the
  function that opens or closes it from the page's own code.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close attaches its panel with `attachPanel`, and the README lists
  it among the controls.
- **Tests.** Browser tests open and close a panel by its button and its Close, by keyboard and
  pointer, with focus back on the button, and attach a button twice to one function.
