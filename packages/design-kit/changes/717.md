The kit's browser tests draw every place focus can be against every piece that reads it, and the
reference says what holds in each.

- **Docs.** The reference's "Where focus can be" and record 19 say what each piece reads on the
  page, in shadow roots open and closed and in frames of the page's origin and of another, and where
  a page reads focus short of where it is.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox runs each place focus can be against
  each piece that reads it and names each square that fails with its cause and the answer it gives.
