A row can hold a group of buttons at its end, which moves under its text where the row has no room
for both.

- **Sheets.** `dk-row-actions` groups a row's buttons at its end, in order. The text keeps room for
  about twelve characters of its own size beside the group, and where the row has less, the group
  moves under the text to the row's end, so a name reads across at twice the text size on a phone.
- **Docs.** The reference shows a row with a group of buttons and says when to use one, and the
  README lists it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests keep a row's title on one line with its group under it on a 320px and a
  390px phone at twice the text size, the group beside the text in the middle of the row at the
  default size, and its buttons reached in order by the keyboard.
