A dialog's corner Close is centered on its title's first line on a wide page, and focus moves past
it to what comes next.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-dialog-inset` is how far a dialog's content stands in, `--dk-space-5-held`, and
  `--dk-space-4-held` at 48rem wide or less and on a page with an app's frame or a tab bar's page.
  A dialog pads by it, and its corner Close is placed from it.
- **Sheets.** A dialog's corner Close is centered on its title's first line at every width where
  the dialog's padding leaves it room, where on a page wider than 48rem it stood 8px above it.
- **Controls.** As a dialog opens on its corner Close, `attachDialog` moves focus to the first thing
  after the Close that takes focus, a field before the answers included, passing over one that
  cannot, such as a disabled answer, and to the title where nothing can. A title it gives a place in
  the order gives that place back as the dialog closes.
- **Docs.** The reference's tokens name `--dk-dialog-inset`.
- **Tests.** A browser test holds a dialog's and a panel's Close to its title's first line at 320
  and 1280 wide. Browser tests open a dialog with a field before its answers and find focus on the
  field, and one whose answer is disabled and find focus on the title, and find a title that had no
  place in the order keeps none once its dialog closes.
