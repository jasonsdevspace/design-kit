A dialog's corner Close is centered on its title's first line on a wide page, and focus moves past
it to the first field or answer.

- **Tokens.** `--dk-dialog-inset` is how far a dialog's content stands in, `--dk-space-5-held`, and
  `--dk-space-4-held` at 48rem wide or less and in a dialog on a page with an app's frame or a tab
  bar's page. A dialog pads by it, and its corner Close is placed from it.
- **Sheets.** A dialog's corner Close is centered on its title's first line at every width where
  the dialog's padding leaves it room, where on a page wider than 48rem it stood 8px above it.
- **Controls.** As a dialog opens on its corner Close, `attachDialog` moves focus to the first field
  or answer after it that takes focus, a radio group's checked radio where it has one, passing over
  one that cannot, such as a disabled answer, and a link or button among the dialog's words, so a
  long dialog opens at its top. Where nothing can, the title takes focus, and a title the page gave
  no place in the order gives back the one it borrows as focus leaves it.
- **Docs.** The reference's tokens name `--dk-dialog-inset`.
- **Tests.** Browser tests hold a dialog's and a panel's Close to its title's first line at 320 and
  1280 wide, and a dialog on a page with an app to a phone's padding at 1280 wide. They open a
  dialog with a field before its answers, one with a radio group, one whose answer is disabled and
  one with no answers, and find focus on the field, the checked radio and the title, and a title
  that had no place in the order keeps none once its dialog closes.
