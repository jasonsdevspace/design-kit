A stepper's field keeps its stepper inside it in a row of the page's own.

- **Sheets.** A `dk-field` holding a `dk-stepper`, not inline, is never narrower than the stepper
  with its buttons at their least width, so in a page's own flex row beside a text field the
  stepper stays inside its field. Its label, hint and error break their words anywhere they must, so
  a long word in them sets no least width. A stepper's button asks for the target where there is
  room and for its least width where there is not, so a stepper's least width is its buttons' least
  and its value's. The field's least width rests on `:has()`, and without it the field keeps none.
- **Docs.** The reference says a stepper's field is never narrower than the stepper at its least
  width and that its words break anywhere they must.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a stepper field beside a text field in a page's own flex row at
  320 wide and twice the text size and finds the stepper and both its buttons inside the field, at
  least the target at the usual text size wide and the target tall.
