A button can be busy while its press is carried out, ignoring presses at once and showing and saying so only once the wait is long enough to notice.

- **Sheets.** A `.dk-button[data-busy]` is drawn off only once the wait outlasts `--dk-wait`, by a transition delay that reduced motion and forced colors keep, and shows `cursor: progress` at once.
- **Controls.** `attachBusy(button, { status, say })` stops a press of a busy button before the page's handlers, says the button's `data-busy` words, or "Saving.", once on the status line after `--dk-wait`, and drops words not yet said when the button stops being busy.
- **React.** `Button` takes `busy`, true or the words to say, which writes `aria-disabled` and `data-busy` and runs no `onClick`, and `useBusy()` attaches a busy button, speaking on the screen's `StatusProvider`.
- **Docs.** The reference's Buttons section shows the busy button, drawn from a new example, and says when a button is pressed, off or busy. Record 13 sets the controls' budget at 12 KB by its rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests press a busy button again and find one save, find the pointer's wait at once, the look held and nothing said for a fast save, the look changed and the words said once after the wait with motion reduced or not, the look held in forced colors, and a React double press saving once.
