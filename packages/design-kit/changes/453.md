A button can be busy while its press is carried out, ignoring presses at once and showing and saying so only once the wait is long enough to notice.

- **Sheets.** A `.dk-button[data-busy]` is drawn off only once the wait outlasts `--dk-wait`, by a transition delay that reduced motion and forced colors keep, and shows `cursor: progress` at once.
- **Controls.** `attachBusy(button, { status, say })` marks a busy button `aria-disabled` where the page has not, stops a press by pointer, Enter, Space or a form's Enter before the button's own handlers, says the button's `data-busy` words, or "Saving.", once on the status line after the wait the sheet holds its look back by, drops words not yet said when the work is done, and starts a wait of its own for a busy that starts again at once. A `Say` takes words as a function, read as they are put on the line, so words no longer true are not said.
- **React.** `Button` takes `busy`, true or the words to say, which writes `aria-disabled` and `data-busy` and runs no `onClick`, and `useBusy()` attaches a busy button, speaking on the screen's `StatusProvider`.
- **Docs.** The reference's Buttons section shows the busy button, drawn from a new example, and says when a button is pressed, off or busy.
- **Tests.** Browser tests press a busy button again by pointer, Enter, Space and a form's Enter and find one save, find the attach's mark and the pointer's wait, the look held and nothing said for a fast save, words dropped for work done just after the wait, the look changed and the words said once after it with motion reduced or not, words said again for a busy that starts again, the look held in forced colors, and in React a double press saving once and a save done just after the wait saying nothing late.
