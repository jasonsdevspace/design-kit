A landing page draws its bands, a column of pieces in a grid and a group of sections from the kit,
with no override of the content page's flow.

- **Sheets.** `dk-band` lines a band the width of the screen up with the column and spaces it by the
  flow, with `--dk-space-7` above and below it. `dk-stack` is the flow with no room of its own, for
  a column in a grid's cell. `dk-sections` sets sections side by side, each with a page section's
  heading and steps, a section's step from what comes before it.
- **Docs.** The reference shows a landing page in bands with a hero's grid and a group of sections,
  and the README lists the pieces.
- **Tests.** Browser tests line a band up with the column and space it by the flow, space a grid in
  a band by its gap alone and a stack by the flow, and set a group two to a row on a wide page and
  one on a phone, each section with a page section's heading and steps.
