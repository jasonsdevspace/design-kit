A landing page draws its bands, a column of pieces in a grid and a group of sections from the kit,
with no override of the content page's flow.

- **Sheets.** `dk-band` lines a band the width of the screen up with the column and spaces it by the
  flow, with `--dk-band-room` above and below it, `--dk-space-7` until a page sets it, and holds its
  prose to the measure. `dk-stack` is the flow with no room of its own, for a column in a grid's
  cell. `dk-sections` sets sections side by side, each with a page section's heading and steps, a
  section's step from what comes before it, or the flow's step under the heading that names it. A
  content page's sections keep their room under its headings. The corner Close's rules check their
  container before they look ahead for the Close, so a page with a long list restyles in less time
  in Chromium.
- **Docs.** The reference shows a landing page in bands with a hero's grid and a group of sections,
  drawn from its example, lists `--dk-band-room` among the properties a page sets, and the README
  lists the pieces. Record 17 is amended for them.
- **Tests.** Browser tests line a band up with the column and space it by the flow, space a grid in
  a band by its gap alone and a stack by the flow, and set a group two to a row on a wide page and
  one on a phone, each section with a page section's heading and steps. They hold a band's prose to
  the measure, keep a content page's section a section's step under its heading, reach a row and a
  switch past a band's column, hold a band's sides on an app's page, and keep every word whole on a
  320px phone at twice the text size.
