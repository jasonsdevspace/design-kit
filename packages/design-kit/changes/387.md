An app's frame keeps its sides at no more than 16px at any text size, so its words keep their room
on a phone.

- **Upgrading.** A page that lines its own content up with an app frame's sides, or with a toast's
  words, by `--dk-space-4` moves to `--dk-space-4-held`, which matches them at any text size. A
  page that set `--dk-space-4` or its root font size above 16px finds a frame's sides, a toast's
  and the band insets on an app's page held at 16px.
- **Tokens.** On a page with an app's frame or a tab bar, `--dk-band-left` and `--dk-band-right`
  step in by `--dk-space-4-held`, so the skip link and a demo's strip start where the frame's words
  do.
- **Sheets.** An app's bar, body, toast place and dock pad their sides by `--dk-space-4-held`, which
  is 16px at the default text size as before, and a toast stands that far from a page's sides and
  pads its words by it.
- **Docs.** The reference's frame and its token table say the frame's sides are held.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find the frame's sides at 16px at the default text size and on a 320px
  phone at twice the text size, and the skip link starting where the frame's words do there.
