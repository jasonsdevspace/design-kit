A stepper, a form and a group of cells the page attaches inside a frame of its own origin work there
as they do on the page, and a focus request asked from inside a frame leaves a visitor's move in it
alone.

- **Controls.** `attachForm` on a form inside such a frame checks and refuses it, where it let the
  submit through with nothing checked, and focus moves to the summary or the field in error in the
  frame. `attachStepper` there keeps a button that has focus shown as its value turns read only and
  hands focus to its value or its box in the frame as it is taken down, where the button hid and
  focus fell to the frame's body. `attachRoving` there hands focus on to the next cell where the one
  with focus is taken out. The form and the group tell the frame's controls by their names rather
  than the page's classes. A focus request keeps where focus was inside the frame and compares where
  focus is as that frame's document sees it, so a visitor's move between two of its controls counts
  as a move, and a body no one can type in, the frame's or the page's around it, still counts as
  free. Its id and the home it falls back to are still looked for on the page. A field's language
  is read past a shadow root inside a frame as it is past one on the page.
- **Docs.** The reference's form, stepper, grid and focus request sections say how each works inside
  a frame the page drives, record 19's decision covers the stepper, the form, the group and a
  request asked from a frame, and record 13 sets the `attachForm` budget again by its rule.
- **Tests.** Browser tests attach a form, a stepper and a grid inside a frame from the page and find
  a refused submit moving focus to the field in error, a stepper's focused button kept as its value
  turns read only and focus handed to its value or its box as it is taken down, and focus handed on
  from a cell taken out. A browser test finds a request asked from one control of a frame leaving
  focus on another the visitor moved to, and a unit test reads a field's language past a shadow root
  of a frame's.
