A dialog whose opener is gone hands focus to the main content rather than the page's body.

- **Controls.** Where a dialog's opener, `returnTo`, what the browser handed focus to and the frames around the opener all take no focus, the dialog hands focus to the app frame's body or the page's main content, as the other pieces do.
- **Docs.** The reference's dialog section says where focus goes once nothing nearer takes it.
- **Tests.** The focus grid gains a row for a frame the page takes away while a piece is open and a column for a dialog whose opener the page removes, and the dialog's squares there find focus on the main content, or on the frame the opener sat in while that frame is still on the page, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
