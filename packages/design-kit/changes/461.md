A band can lay out a hero's words beside its picture with a kit class alone, stacked on a phone.

- **Sheets.** `dk-band-hero` holds the words' stack and then the picture, one over the other `--dk-space-6` apart on a phone and side by side, centered on each other, from 48rem. The picture is `--dk-band-hero-picture` wide, 28rem until a page sets it, and never past 45% of the row, so the words keep room for a display heading. An `img`, a `picture` or a `video` in it fills its cell and keeps its proportions, cropped rather than stretched.
- **Docs.** The reference's band example uses the hero in place of the grid it wrote by hand, its band section says how the hero lays out and why the words come first, and the properties table names `--dk-band-hero-picture`.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium and WebKit find the words beside the picture from 48rem, centered and a step apart, the picture at 28rem, at the width a page names and held to 45% of the row, a display heading at 48rem broken between words only, the words over the picture a step apart on a phone with the picture in its own proportions, the picture cropped to fill beside taller words, nothing pushed sideways on a 320px phone at twice the text size or under wider spacing, and no audit findings in both themes and in forced colors.
