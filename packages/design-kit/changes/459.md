A field, a select and a stepper can be read only, drawn as a value to read rather than a control that is off.

- **Sheets.** `.dk-input[readonly]` and `.dk-textarea[readonly]` keep their edge's width, drawn clear on a clear ground with the text in the page's color, before the error's edge, so a read-only field with an error still shows it, and a disabled one is drawn disabled. The edge takes the ground's color in forced colors.
- **Controls.** `attachStepper` hides a stepper's buttons while its field is read only, keeping a focused button, marked off, until focus leaves it, follows a field that turns read only or disabled as it changes, and brings the buttons back when it can change again. A stepper drawn by hand with no buttons and a read-only value is left as it is.
- **React.** `SelectField` takes `readOnly`, drawing a read-only field with the chosen option's words, found inside groups too, and a hidden field for each value, passing its `disabled`, `form`, `title` and `autoFocus` on.
- **Docs.** The reference's Fields section shows read-only fields, drawn from a new example, and says when a field is read only, off, busy or disabled, in place of telling a field a visitor cannot change to be disabled.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find the read-only look against a field that can be changed and one that is disabled in both themes, the edge in forced colors, a read-only select's words and the value its form sends, a read-only stepper with no buttons, a stepper whose field turns read only keeping its focused button, no audit findings, and every field whole at 320px with twice the text size and under wider text spacing.
