A stepper drawn again and again in one checked form leaves none of its fields held by the form.

- **Controls.** An attached stepper taken down once its field has left the page has every form the
  kit checks that held the field let go of it, and takes its `aria-invalid="false"` away, whoever
  set it, so a stepper drawn again and again in one long-lived form, as one keyed by a round, leaves
  no field held. A field still in the page keeps its mark until its form is let go, so a stepper
  taken down and attached again keeps it, and one taken down before its form loses it with the form.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper section and record 21 say when a stepper taken down has its
  form let go of its field and take its mark away.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox take a stepper down and then its form and
  find no mark on it, keep the mark of a stepper taken down and attached again on the same field,
  draw a stepper again and again in one form, each leaving the page, and find the form's teardown
  reaches none of them, let go of a field the form marked itself and of one moved between two
  checked forms once it leaves the page, and leave the mark a later check of a form sets on a
  stepper that outlived the first.
