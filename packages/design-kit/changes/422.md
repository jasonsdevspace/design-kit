A toast hands focus back when its Close or its action is pressed by pointer or tap in Safari.

- **Controls.** `attachToast` gives a toast's Close and its action `tabindex="0"` where the page's
  markup has none, and keeps one the page set, so Safari, which focuses a pressed button only where
  it has a tabindex, leaves focus in the toast on a press, and focus goes back where it came from as
  the toast leaves, as a press by key does.
- **React.** `Toast` draws its Close and its action with `tabIndex={0}`.
- **Docs.** The reference's toast says why its buttons carry a tabindex.
- **Tests.** Browser tests press a toast's Close and its action by pointer, in Chromium and WebKit,
  and find focus back where it was.
