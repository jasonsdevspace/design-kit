A plain form spaces a button or a callout after a field as it spaces fields, and a field's long
words break inside its column.

- **Sheets.** A form with no class attribute stands each part after the first `--dk-space-4` below
  the one before it, a button, a row of actions or a callout as well as a field, where before only a
  field after a field stood apart and a button sat flush under the control above it. A hidden input,
  a hidden part or an empty live callout before the first part adds no room. `.dk-field` holds its
  one column to the width it is given, so a word with no place to break, such as the email address
  in the kit's own error, breaks inside the field at 320 wide and twice the text size rather than
  hanging past its edge.
- **Docs.** The reference says how a plain form spaces its parts and that a field holds to its
  column.
- **Tests.** Browser tests stand the README's checkout buttons and callout one step under what is
  above them, with an empty or hidden callout adding no room, keep a field with the kit's email
  error inside a narrow form at twice the text size, and pass over a hidden input before a plain
  form's first part.
