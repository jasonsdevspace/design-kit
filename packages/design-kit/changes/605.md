A plain form spaces a button, a row of actions or a callout after a field as it spaces fields, and
a field's long words break inside its column.

- **Upgrading.** A form with no class attribute that a page lays out itself, as a row or a grid, now
  has room above every part after the first that starts its own line, not only a field after a
  field, so give it a class.
- **Sheets.** A form with no class attribute stands each part after the first `--dk-space-4` below
  the one before it, a button, a row of actions, a callout or a paragraph as well as a field, where
  before only a field after a field stood apart and a button sat flush under the control above it.
  Its parts' browser margins are taken away, as the flow takes them in a panel or a dialog, so a
  heading or a paragraph in it stands the same step. A hidden input, a hidden or visually hidden
  part, an empty live callout, a script, a style, a template or a noscript before the first part
  adds no room. Parts on one line take none, so two buttons side by side, or a label, the error the
  kit adds and a control written bare, keep their places. `.dk-field` holds its one column to the
  width it is given, so a word with no place to break, such as the email address in the kit's own
  error, breaks inside the field at 320 wide and twice the text size rather than hanging past its
  edge.
- **Docs.** The reference says how a plain form spaces its parts, what adds no room and what keeps
  its line, and that a field holds to its column. The README's checkout holds its two buttons in a
  row of actions.
- **Tests.** Browser tests stand the README's checkout row and callout `--dk-space-4` under what is
  above them, with an empty or hidden callout adding no room, keep a field with the kit's email
  error inside a narrow form at twice the text size, space a lone button after a field, a heading
  and a paragraph by the same step, keep two buttons side by side and a label beside its input on
  one line, keep the error the kit adds to bare markup near its label and control, and add no room
  for a hidden input, a script or a template before a plain form's first part.
