A plain form spaces a row of actions or a callout as it spaces its fields, and a field's long words
break inside its column.

- **Upgrading.** A form with no class attribute that a page lays out as a row, holding a row of
  actions or a callout after a field, now has room above them, so give it a class.
- **Sheets.** In a form with no class attribute, a field, a row of actions or a callout that is not
  empty stands `--dk-space-4` below what sits above it, once another of those has come before it,
  wherever a hidden input or a script sits between them, where before only a field after a field
  stood apart and the README's buttons sat flush under the select above them. Only the kit's own
  blocks are spaced, so a page's own markup in a plain form draws as it would in a form with a
  class, and a block out of sight or an empty callout counts as none. `.dk-field` gives its one
  column no least width, so a word with no place to break, such as the email address in the kit's
  own error, breaks inside the field at 320 wide and twice the text size rather than hanging past
  its edge, in a narrow form and beside a button in a page's own row.
- **Docs.** The README's checkout and the reference's hold their two buttons in a row of actions.
  The reference says which blocks a plain form spaces and that a field holds to its column, and
  record 24 gives the choice and the options turned down.
- **Tests.** Browser tests stand the README's checkout row and a filled callout `--dk-space-4` under
  what is above them, with an empty or hidden callout adding no room, stand each of the kit's blocks
  a step apart in a plain form and inside a dialog, draw a page's own markup in a plain form as in a
  form with a class, keep a field with the kit's email error inside a narrow form and beside a
  button in a page's own row at twice the text size, and leave the error a refusal adds to bare
  markup at its own margins.
