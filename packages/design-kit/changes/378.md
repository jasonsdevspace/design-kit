A panel and the tiles in it hold their inset on a phone at any text size.

- **Upgrading.** A page that set `--dk-space-4` above 16px to widen a phone's panels and tiles sees
  them padded 16px, since the card's inset on a phone is now the held step. Such a page sets
  `--dk-card-inset` in a query of its own instead.
- **Tokens.** `--dk-card-inset` is `--dk-space-4-held` at 48rem wide or less, 1rem and no more than
  16px at any text size, where it grew with the text, as a dialog's and an app column's insets
  already hold. On a page wider than 48rem it is `--dk-space-5` as before, and at the default text
  size nothing moves. A panel's corner Close keeps its ring inside the panel where the held padding
  leaves no room above it at a large text size, and sits a little below the title's first line.
- **Sheets.** The rule that held a tile's inset inside a panel on a phone is gone, since the card's
  inset now holds it for a panel and every tile alike.
- **Docs.** The reference's panels and tokens say the card inset is held on a phone.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws tiles in a plain and a raised panel and on the page at 320 wide
  and twice the text size, finds no word drawn across two lines or cut off, and holds a panel, a
  tile in it and a tile on the page to 16px.
