A click on a control below a stepper lands, though the stepper's error would move the control.

- **Controls.** An attached stepper holds back the error of a change, or of letters as focus leaves
  them, while a press on a control anywhere outside the stepper is down, as a Save button below
  it, one tied to the form from outside it or a button beside a stepper in no form, until the press
  ends, however long it is held, so the error drawn above never moves the control from under the
  pointer and the click and the submit it makes land. A submit the form refuses then shows the
  stepper's error and says one message, a press dragged off and let go without a click shows the
  error once it ends, a press that turns into a drag shows it as the drag starts, and focus leaving
  by the keyboard shows it at once.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper section and record 21 say when a stepper holds back its error
  for a press outside it, and why it reads the press from what is active rather than from a
  listener.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, in a form attached with `attachForm` and
  one refused through `useFormRefusal`, type a value past the range above a Save button, press and
  let go of Save with the pointer, and find the form refused on that one click with one message, as
  for a Save button outside the form tied to it, and in Chromium and WebKit for one tap. They keep
  the click of a button that submits nothing below the stepper and of one beside a stepper in no
  form, show the error at once as Tab leaves the stepper, once a press on Save is dragged off and
  let go and once a press on a link turns into a drag, and save on a press after an error is put
  right.
