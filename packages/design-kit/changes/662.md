A click on a button below a stepper lands, though the stepper's error would move the button.

- **Controls.** An attached stepper holds back the error of a change, or of letters as focus leaves
  them, while a press outside the stepper is down in its form, as on a Save button below it, until
  the press ends, so the error drawn above never moves the button from under the pointer and the
  click and the submit it makes land. A submit the form refuses then shows the stepper's error and
  says one message, a press dragged off and let go without a click shows the error once it ends,
  and focus leaving by the keyboard shows it at once.
- **Docs.** The reference's stepper section and record 21 say when a stepper holds back its error
  for a press below it, and why it reads the press from what is active rather than from a listener.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, in a form attached with `attachForm` and
  one refused through `useFormRefusal`, type a value past the range above a Save button, press and
  let go of Save with the pointer, and find the form refused on that one click with one message.
  They show the error at once as Tab leaves the stepper, and once a press on Save is dragged off and
  let go.
