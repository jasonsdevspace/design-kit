A picture of a frame or of a page that ends in a tab bar is as tall as what it holds.

- **Sheets.** A frame or a page that ends in a tab bar that carries `dk-picture` or sits inside an
  element with it is as tall as what it holds, where it was the screen's height, and its tab bar
  sits at its foot in its place rather than held to the visitor's screen, so a page draws a picture
  of a screen with no rule of its own for its size.
- **Docs.** The reference's app frame says a pictured frame is as tall as what it holds and the page
  draws its edge, corners and room.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a pictured frame and a pictured page that ends in a tab bar, the
  class on a wrapper and on each, on a tall and a short screen, and holds each to what it holds and
  its tab bar to its foot.
