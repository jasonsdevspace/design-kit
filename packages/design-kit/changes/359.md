A picture of a frame or of a page that ends in a tab bar is as tall as what it holds.

- **Upgrading.** A page that pictures a frame or a page that ends in a tab bar with `dk-picture`
  draws it as tall as what it holds, where it was the screen's height, so a page that set only an
  edge or corners on it and wants it taller sets a least height of its own.
- **Tokens.** `--dk-safe-top`, `--dk-safe-right`, `--dk-safe-bottom` and `--dk-safe-left` are none
  inside an element with `dk-picture`, since a picture touches no edge of the visitor's screen.
- **Sheets.** A frame or a page that ends in a tab bar that carries `dk-picture` or sits inside an
  element with it is as tall as what it holds, where it was the screen's height, and its tab bar
  sits at its foot in its place rather than held to the visitor's screen or raised over the page,
  so a page draws a picture of a screen with no rule of its own for its size.
- **Docs.** The reference's app frame and tab bar say a pictured frame or page is as tall as what it
  holds, its bar in its place, and the page draws its edge, corners and room.
- **Tests.** A browser test draws a pictured frame with its dock and tab row and a pictured page that
  ends in a tab bar on a page with safe areas, on a phone and a wide screen, and holds each to what
  it holds, its tab bar to its foot in its place and none of the room a phone keeps at its edges.
  The sheets' budget stays 10.75 KB, with the stack measured whole at 10,695 bytes and a median of 193.
