An empty live callout stays out of sight and out of the flow in every supported browser.

- **Upgrading.** A page that writes a live callout by hand marks it `data-empty` while it is empty and has no actions, and takes the mark away as its words or actions come, so the flow passes over it. `Callout` does this itself.
- **Sheets.** A callout marked `data-empty` is drawn as visually hidden text is by a rule with no `:has()`, which Firefox 120 lacks, and the flow's step, the short step under a heading and the room kept for a toast pass over it, so a list after one starts where it would with none.
- **React.** `Callout` marks itself `data-empty` while it is live with no title, message or actions, unless the page marks it.
- **Docs.** The reference's live callout steps mark the empty callout, take the mark away as words or actions come, and mark it again once emptied.
- **Tests.** A browser test, with every `:has()` rule taken out as a browser without it drops them, finds an empty live callout out of sight, a list after it in an app's body where it starts with none, and the room for a toast kept under the list.
