An empty live callout stays out of sight and out of the flow in every supported browser.

- **Sheets.** A callout marked `data-empty` is drawn as visually hidden text is with no `:has()`, which Firefox 120 lacks, and the flow's step and the room kept for a toast pass over it, so a list after one starts where it would with none.
- **React.** `Callout` marks itself `data-empty` while it is live with no title, message or actions.
- **Docs.** The reference's live callout steps mark the empty callout and take the mark away as the words come.
- **Tests.** A browser test finds a list after an empty live callout in an app's body where it starts with none, and that the rule hiding it asks for no `:has()`.
