The kit's words for a failure live in an internal module of their own, with unit tests in plain
Node.

- **Controls.** The words for each failure, the dated limits among them, and the lookups they share
  with the controls move into two internal modules behind the controls entry, which no entry
  exports. No words change. Every function a page bundles comes to 89 bytes more and `attachForm`
  alone to 28 more, gzipped, since a bundler puts an imported module's code ahead of the controls,
  beyond gzip's window from the form code the words share text with. The module imported bare and
  `attachTable` alone are unchanged.
- **Docs.** Record 13 gives the controls' size before and after the move, and record 21 names the
  unit tests.
- **Tests.** Unit tests give the kit's words for every failure and every dated limit the browser
  tests read, and an ISO week, a year under a hundred, a fraction of a second, an overnight window
  and a malformed language.
