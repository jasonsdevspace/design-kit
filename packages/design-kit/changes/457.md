A page can draw a split row, a note and a large numeral, and narrow a group of sections, with kit classes alone.

- **Sheets.** `dk-split` sets a title at the start and a figure or a control at the end on one line, on their baseline, and the end under the start a small step apart where they do not fit, with no room of their own, each piece breaking a word too long for the line, and holds to the measure with the prose around it. `dk-note` is the muted text at the small size. `dk-numeral` is `--dk-text-numeral` in the mono face with tabular figures, at the strong weight on a line no taller than the figure, never broken, and no wider on a phone at a large text size than five figures fit. `--dk-sections-least` sets the least width of a section in `dk-sections`, 20rem until a page sets it.
- **Docs.** The reference has a section for the split row, the note and the numeral, drawn from a new example of a player's score. It says a numeral carries its unit for a screen reader, a numeral that changes is said on the status line rather than made live, and a number in a row stays a `dk-row-figure`. The properties table names `--dk-sections-least`, and the numeral token's comment names the class that sets its face.
