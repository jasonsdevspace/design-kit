A busy button shows that it is at work once the wait has passed, not only that it is off.

- **Sheets.** A busy button, once `--dk-wait` has passed, draws a short row of dots centered at its
  foot as its `::after`. They pulse, stand still under reduced motion, take the button's color or
  the system's in forced colors, take no place in the row and hold no words, so the button keeps its
  size and its name.
- **React.** `Button`'s `busy` describes the dots.
- **Docs.** "A busy button" in the reference describes the dots and no longer says there is no
  spinner. Record 2 lets a busy button follow its `:where()` with `::after`, the one pseudo-element
  a component draws of its own.
- **Tests.** Browser tests find a busy button past the wait drawing the dots, which an off button
  beside it lacks, with its name kept and neither button's box changed by them, the dots out of
  sight during the wait, and still under reduced motion. The scope test allows the busy button's
  `::after` and no other.
