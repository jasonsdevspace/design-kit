A callout beside a field ends where the fields end, in a form or not.

- **Sheets.** In a page, a band or a stack, a callout written before or after a field or a row of
  fields in what holds them holds to `--dk-field-measure` as they do, so a page that draws its
  fields with no form keeps one right edge, where only a callout inside a form did. A callout with
  no field beside it there runs the column as before.
- **Docs.** The reference's field section says which callouts end where the fields end, and that a
  callout reads `--dk-field-measure` from what holds it. The accessibility notes say a callout
  before the first field runs the column without `:has()`.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium and WebKit finds a callout before the first field of a
  stack, one between two fields and one after a row of fields ending at their right edge, one after
  a field and a table ending at the measure as the prose there does, one with no field beside it
  running the column, one in a dialog as wide as the dialog's field, and every one but the callout
  before the first field held the same without `:has()`.
