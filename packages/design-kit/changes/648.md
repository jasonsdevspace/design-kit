A date field's words stand clear of its picker's icon on a phone at twice the text size.

- **Sheets.** On a screen under 12rem wide, which only a large text size makes of a phone, a
  `dk-input` for a date, a month or a week pads its start by `--dk-space-1` and its end not at all,
  so in Chromium, which draws a picker's icon at the field's end, the words end before it at 320
  wide and twice the text size, where they ran under it. A field at the usual text size is drawn as
  before.
- **Docs.** The reference's field section gives the narrower padding and where it applies, and the
  accessibility notes say a date with a time is still cut short on that screen, as a dated field in
  a panel there is, and one in a typeface wider than the tests draw.
- **Tests.** A browser test in Chromium at 320 wide and twice the text size finds a date's, a
  month's and a week's words ending before the icon with no sideways scroll, and one in Chromium,
  WebKit and Firefox finds every kind of dated field padded as a text field at the usual size.
