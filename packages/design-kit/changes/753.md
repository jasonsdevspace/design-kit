The focus grid builds a frame of the page's origin inside one of another origin only once the kit hears it.

- **Tests.** The grid waits for the outer frame's load as well as the inner frame's page before a square runs, since the kit hears the inner frame at the outer frame's load, so a press there is no longer made before it is heard.
- **Docs.** Record 19 says Firefox does not hear a press in a frame of the page's origin inside one of another origin before that outer frame's load.
- **Tests.** A browser test brings a view by a press in such a frame while the outer frame's load is held, and finds the view's heading focused in Chromium and WebKit. It is marked as failing in Firefox, which misses the press.
