A button whose face is an icon alone draws its icon large enough to read, and a tab bar's icons are
stroked no heavier than their labels.

- **Upgrading.** A button whose face is an icon alone adds `dk-button-icon`, or takes `icon` on
  `Button`, to draw its icon at 1.25rem. Without it the icon stays at the button's text size, as
  before.
- **Sheets.** `dk-button-icon` on a button draws its `dk-icon` at 1.25rem, and a tab bar's icon is
  stroked at `max(1.5px, 0.125rem)`.
- **React.** `Button` takes `icon`, which adds `dk-button-icon`, with an action or a toggle.
- **Docs.** The reference's icons show the star toggle with `dk-button-icon` and say when to add it.
- **Tests.** Browser tests draw an icon-only button's icon at 20px and 40px at twice the text size,
  and a tab bar's stroke at 2px and 4px.
