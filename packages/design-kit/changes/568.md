A control outlives a takedown and an attach of its element in the same task, so a hook's ref made afresh on every render, a ref that merges two hooks and an element passed from one hook's ref to another's keep their control, a form's check and a busy wait included, as record 23 has it.

- **Controls.** Each signal an attach of an element is given holds its control, and the control is
  taken down a microtask after the last of them aborts, unless the element is attached again by
  then, which joins it and keeps its state and the parts it found first. Until then the control is
  whole, so a call in the abort's own task, such as a panel's `set` or a dialog's opener, still
  acts, and a page that means to start afresh within the task calls the function the attach
  returned, which takes the control down at once, or attaches after a microtask. A control attached
  again returns what its first attach returned and runs on the options of its newest holder, or of
  the holder whose options apply where that one gives new ones, and when the holder whose options
  apply lets go, the newest one left that gave options takes over with them a microtask later,
  unless a newer holder has given its own by then. A call with no signal made while a takedown
  waits, with no signal joined since, lands it at once and attaches afresh, and an attach made from
  inside a control's own takedown gets a control of its own. Options that give only a `signal` count
  as none, so a dialog attached again with only its `signal`, as through a ref made afresh, keeps
  `stay`, and a call with no signal is kept under the page's own hold where it has one, so a hook
  sharing the element takes nothing of the page's update with it as it goes. A toast hands focus
  back only where focus is still in it, or where focus is free, on the body or nowhere, and the
  toast had it as it left the page, so focus a visitor put anywhere else is never taken. A toast
  taken from the page with focus in it before its takedown lands hands focus back, in Chromium,
  which fires focusout as it goes, as in WebKit and Firefox, and a toast reads whether focus is in
  it a microtask after focus leaves, so focus that leaves and comes back in one task keeps it shown
  and one moved in the page starts its wait again, and focus taken to a control outside the toast
  stays there as the toast is taken away. A copy button joined with a source that gives the element
  its last press copied keeps the word it shows, reads the source only while a word is shown and
  takes one that throws for another element, and progress counts its steps again only where their
  count changes.
- **React.** The hooks take their part down at once, their ref's cleanup aborting the element's signal and dropping it in one call, and a ref called with null does so for every element. `useRoving` and `useProgress` pass their new options with the element's own signal, so they apply to that hook where two share an element. `StatusProvider` keeps no controller of its own.
- **Docs.** Record 23 states the rule and why deferring the takedown in the hooks was turned down, and records 13, 15, 20 and 22 say what it changes. The reference's section on taking a control down stands on its own, names `moveFocus`, says when a takedown lands and how options follow the newest holder, and each attach links it. The doc comments on `./controls` and the hooks say what the code does now, and a panel's button taken from the page without being taken down is said to stay held until it is.
- **Tests.** The helper's unit tests find an abort's takedown waits a microtask, a signal held
  before then keeps the control, a call with no signal holding only where nothing else does, the
  detach taking it down at once with no listener left on any signal, the options of the newest
  holder that gave any applied as holders come and go, a newer holder joined in the same task
  keeping its own, a call with no signal landing a waiting takedown, and an attach from inside a
  takedown getting a control of its own. Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox abort and
  attach every piece in one task and find each kept with its state, attach after a microtask and
  find each afresh, hand focus back from a toast taken down and removed in one task but leave it on
  a control outside the toast that focus moved to, start a piece afresh where it is attached with no
  signal in its holder's task, leave focus a visitor moved after a toast left the page, keep a
  dialog staying through an attach that gives only a signal, keep a toast shown while focus leaves
  and comes back in one task, put a copy button's word back where a source reading the page's state
  gives another block or throws, keep a progress step heard through a join with the same count, and
  join each piece again and again with nothing growing and nothing left once it is taken down. In
  Chromium a toast moved in the page with focus in it starts its wait again. Under `StrictMode`, a
  form, a row of cells and a busy button behind refs made afresh keep their check, their observer
  and their wait, a busy wait and a shared row keep through a merged ref, a form and a row passed
  between hooks take the new hook's options, and either of two form hooks dropped leaves the other's
  check and refusal. Tests that called a piece in the abort's task now call it once the takedown
  lands, and the tests of a later signal held while the first aborts now find the control kept.
