- **Controls.** Each signal an attach of an element is given holds its control, and the control is taken down a microtask after the last of them aborts, unless the element is attached again by then, which joins it and keeps its state and the parts it found first. A call with no signal holds the control for the page's life where nothing else holds it, an attach in the abort's own task among them, and an attach made from inside a control's own takedown gets a control of its own. A control attached again returns what its first attach returned and runs on the options of its newest holder, or of the holder whose options apply where that one gives new ones, and when the holder whose options apply lets go, the newest one left that gave options takes over with them a microtask later, unless a newer holder has given its own by then.
