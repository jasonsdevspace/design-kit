A panel whose buttons take no focus hands it to what follows the panel in the order the page draws it.

- **Controls.** Where no button of a closing panel takes focus, focus goes to the first control after the panel in the order Tab follows, through a slot to a control slotted into a widget after its panel and into a following widget's open shadow root, rather than past them to the page after the widget.
- **Docs.** The reference's panel section says what follows a panel is read in the order the page draws it, and record 19 says the walk for it runs only where every button is off.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel inside a widget with its button off and find focus on a page's button slotted after the panel, and on the first control inside a following widget's open shadow root, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
