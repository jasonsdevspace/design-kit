A panel whose buttons take no focus hands it to what follows the panel in the order the page draws it.

- **Controls.** Where no button of a closing panel takes focus, focus goes to the first stop after the panel in the order the page draws it, through a slot to a control slotted into a widget after its panel, into a following widget's open shadow root, to a following frame and to a custom element whose closed shadow root takes focus, rather than past them to the page after the widget. The walk stops at the first stop that takes focus.
- **Docs.** The reference's panel section says what follows a panel is read in the order the page draws it, and record 19 says the walk for it runs only where every button is off.
- **Tests.** Browser tests close a panel inside a widget with its button off and find focus on a page's button slotted after the panel, on the first control inside a following widget's open shadow root, on a following frame and on a following custom element whose closed shadow root takes focus, and past a panel slotted through two widgets, never to a control before it, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
