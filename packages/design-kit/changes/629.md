`focusOnArrival` honors the signal its options take.

- **Controls.** `focusOnArrival(view, { signal })` asks for nothing under a signal already aborted,
  and leaves the press for another view, and an abort calls off a request not yet landed, as the
  function it returns does and as `moveFocus` has it. Before, the signal was taken and never read.
  A request that lands lets go of its signal.
- **Docs.** The reference's sections on taking a control down and on a view's arrival, and record
  22's list of what keeps its contract, name `focusOnArrival`.
- **Tests.** Browser tests in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox find no focus moved and no press spent
  under a signal already aborted, a request called off by an abort with its press handed back, a
  landed request holding nothing on its signal, and the views left landing where one of several
  asking for the same press is aborted.
