The theme switch and the copy button take a `signal` that takes them down, the first to keep the teardown contract record 22 sets for every attach.

- **Controls.** `attachThemeSwitch(element, { signal })` and `attachCopyButton(button, { source, status, signal })` take an `AbortSignal`, and aborting it does what the function they return does. A signal already aborted attaches nothing and leaves the control hidden, a press the clipboard answers after the teardown says nothing, and a control attached again before it is taken down leaves the later call's signal unheard. The type `AttachOptions` carries the option. Both now take themselves down through one internal helper, which no entry exports.
- **Docs.** Record 22 states the teardown contract, with the research and the options weighed, and records 13, 15 and 20 are amended for it. The reference says how a control is taken down and gives the two attaches their `signal`.
- **Tests.** Browser tests attach the theme switch and the copy button with a signal already aborted and find nothing attached, abort a signal and find every listener off and both hidden, find a late clipboard answer and a theme chosen after the teardown change nothing, find an old detach called twice and its signal aborted leave a newer attach alone, and find a later attach's signal unheard. Unit tests hold the helper to running once, whichever of the abort and the detach comes first.
