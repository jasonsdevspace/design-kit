Escape leaves a panel open where Safari ends typing and on its button while it is off, and a close
decides from where focus last moved.

- **Controls.** Escape is left alone where its keydown carries the key code 229, which Safari gives
  the keydown that ends typing in a script such as Japanese. A panel's button that is off does not
  close its own panel by Escape, as it does not by a press, and Escape goes on to a panel around
  it. Where focus moves is now kept at all times, as presses are, so a press in a panel before
  focus moved on no longer decides a later close from code.
- **Docs.** The reference's Escape says so, and its corner Close says again that a page closes a
  panel from a press's `click`. Record 19 adds the button that is off, and record 13 measures the
  controls.
- **Tests.** Browser tests press Escape as Safari ends typing, on a button that is off, and on an off
  button for a closed panel inside an open one, and close a panel from the page's code after a
  press in it and a move of focus.
