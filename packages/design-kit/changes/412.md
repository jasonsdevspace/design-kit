A panel's corner Close hands focus back to its button however it is pressed.

- **Controls.** A press of a panel's corner Close moves focus to the button that opened the panel
  by key, pointer or touch. Safari, Firefox on macOS and iOS do not focus a button a pointer or a
  tap presses, so a Close pressed that way left focus off the page, and with VoiceOver on iOS the
  cursor on what was just hidden. A close from `set` still moves focus only where it was in the
  panel, since the page's code may close it while the visitor is elsewhere.
- **Docs.** The reference's corner Close says the Close hands focus back however it is pressed.
- **Tests.** The panel's browser tests run in WebKit as well, and a close from `set` with focus
  outside the panel leaves focus where it was.
