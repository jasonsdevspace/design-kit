The reference names the contrast a tile's ground holds for each part of the tile.

- **Docs.** The reference says a tile's ground holds the title and the muted detail at 4.5 to 1, and
  the muted sign and the focus ring, drawn in `--dk-focus`, at 3 to 1, in both themes.
- **Tests.** Browser tests measure each tile's title, detail, sign and focus ring as drawn, on the
  grounds a page sets, on the surface and on the grounds a panel steps to, in both themes, since the
  audit never measures the ring.
