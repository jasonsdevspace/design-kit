A tile's ground is named by the contrast it holds.

- **Docs.** The reference says a tile's ground holds the title and the muted detail at 4.5 to 1, and
  the muted sign and the focus ring at 3 to 1, in both themes.
- **Tests.** Browser tests measure each tile's title, detail, sign and focus ring on the grounds a
  page sets, in both themes, where the audit reads only the words.
