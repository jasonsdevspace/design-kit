A panel drawn beside its button inside an open shadow root opens, closes and hands focus back
there, and one inside a closed shadow root opens and closes.

- **Controls.** `attachPanel` looks for a button's panel by its id from the button's own root out
  through each host to the document, as a focus request finds an id, and keeps each panel's state by
  the root that holds it, so a widget's panel and the page's with the same id stay apart. The press,
  Escape and the corner Close are read inside an open shadow root, and on a closed one a button sits
  in, which the kit listens on. Where no button takes focus as the panel closes, focus goes on to
  what follows the panel, past the widget to the page where the widget holds nothing after it.
- **Docs.** The reference and record 19 say a panel's state is kept by the root that holds it, that
  a screen reader follows `aria-controls` within one root only, so a panel belongs in its button's
  root, and that focus goes on past a widget as its panel closes.
- **Tests.** The focus grid's panel squares for an open shadow root and an open shadow root inside a
  frame pass, and the closed shadow root's panel now opens and closes, its focus going to the main
  content as it closes, as the closed root's own reading of focus has it. Browser tests close a
  widget's panel by its Close and by Escape apart from the page's panel with its id, hand focus on
  past the widget, open a widget's panel as its button's markup says once the button before it left,
  close by Escape a page's panel opened from a widget's button before the page drew it, keep the
  page's open panel its own as a widget draws a panel with its id, and open and close a panel inside
  a closed shadow root.
